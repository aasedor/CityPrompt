Gemini sees this text token AND a mask ~21× larger than the nominal area. The archetype

-------------------- THREE COMPETING SHAPE SIGNALS --------------------
Signal A — image: one 3-storey rowhouse, narrow frontage. "Apply the EXACT architectural
  style, materials, and textures from this reference image to the corresponding zone"
  (render.py:439).
Signal B — archetype metadata text: 150m², 3-storey, stoop entry. These are small
  single-dwelling numbers.
Signal C — polygon mask: 3,200m² of editable pixels.

Without a "polygon size is authoritative" rule, Gemini has three reasonable outputs:
  (a) Render a 150m² rowhouse in the corner of the mask, fill the rest with street/yard
      or cream-void (the "tiny building in a large polygon" — the exact failure mode
      that Mapbox commit b9cea11 fixed).
  (b) Render a rowhouse at the archetype's height but stretched 5× wide — disproportioned,
      no interior bays make sense.
  (c) Render ~20 rowhouses tiled across the mask as a block of terraces — reasonable
      urban interpretation but unlikely without an explicit "tile the archetype" cue.

Globe prompt has NO cue selecting among (a)/(b)/(c).

-------------------- WHAT THE MAPBOX FIX 7202d32 DOES (and globe does not) --------------------
Mapbox SCHEMA prompt (post-commit) explicitly says:
  "If the polygon is larger than a typical archetype example, scale it up."

Globe's MANDATORY line (useGlobeAIRender.ts:871) does not contain any such clause.
This is the single most consequential unported fix for scenario 6.

-------------------- DILATION AT LARGE SCALE --------------------
At 80m × 40m, the 8-pixel dilation (~2m) is only 2.5% of polygon width — negligible.
Dilation behaves well here, unlike scenario 5 where it inflated the polygon by 50%.
