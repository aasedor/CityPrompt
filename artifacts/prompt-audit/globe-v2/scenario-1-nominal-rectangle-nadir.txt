-------------------- HEIGHT AT NADIR --------------------
Scale token "3F 12m" is carried at nadir even though at 0° the only height cue is
shadow length + color shift. Nothing in the prompt translates "12m" to "expect a
~6m-long shadow northeast of the footprint". The mask dilates ~2m laterally — not
the shadow direction — so the mask edges cut off the expected shadow.

Height-extension of the mask (line 364-372) extrudes the polygon UPWARD in screen
space. At nadir, "upward" has zero screen displacement — `topPixels ≈ pixels` — so
the height extension collapses and the mask is just the footprint. Consistent, but
means no extra shadow room; Gemini might compress or drop the shadow entirely.
