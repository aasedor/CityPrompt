-------------------- OCCLUSION DELEGATION (279868f PORT STATUS) --------------------
Mapbox commit 279868f: auto-delegates to renderPerZone when
  `partialOcclusion.size > 0`. For ground zones behind buildings this prevents
  relocation.
Globe: buildPrompt injects a PARTIALLY BEHIND A BUILDING hint inline in the zone line
  (useGlobeAIRender.ts:852-854) but NEVER delegates to per-zone — `render()` has no
  `renderPerZoneRef` forwarding logic.

the building cuts across one leg of the L. Without delegation, the single-shot relocation
failure mode Mapbox fixed can still occur on globe. Compounded with the L-shape text
conflicts, scenario 4b is a concrete near-worst-case for globe.
