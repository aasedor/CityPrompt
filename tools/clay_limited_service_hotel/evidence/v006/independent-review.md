# Independent holistic review — clay limited-service hotel, v006 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent (did not build; read only `sources/`, `renders/`, `boards/`). All coordinates are pixels in the named file: renders are 1440x1080, `sources/front.png` 1024x768, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg` 1195x896. Colour values are mean sRGB of the stated box.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the envelope, L-plan, storey count, paired-window grammar, both porte-cocheres, the low wing and the site layout now conform, but the glass lobby tower rises about half to one storey above the main parapet where both sources put its header at or below the parapet line, and the under-parapet accent band is navy on every block where the source pixels show dark green on the charcoal north leg and on the west end of the beige leg.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 2, P2 = 9
