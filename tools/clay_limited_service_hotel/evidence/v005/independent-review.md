# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: clay limited-service hotel, v005

Reviewer: an independent holistic reviewer who did not build this candidate. I judged only the pixels of `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` (read at full resolution, with zoomed crops where noted) and the 4 phone boards. I did not read any build code, reports, README or VERSIONS files.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The building is now clearly the right hotel (4-storey L block, paired windows, glass lobby tower, two stone-pier porte-cocheres, low glazed wing, white roof with RTUs, parking court), but five P1 defects remain visible in the pixels, including a pure-black ground slot carried over from v004.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 6.**

## 4. Camera validity

- **front:** valid low south-east view, and the masses read in the source order (wing, beige block, tower, beige, charcoal). The charcoal block and east canopy are partly occluded by the misplaced pylon sign (P1 #2).
- **left_side, right_side, rear:** valid but distant. The asset fills only about 40% of the frame width. Legible, acceptable as elevation checks.
- **interior:** the label suggests an interior, but the view is an exterior of the lobby taken from the drive. The interior stair and landings are legible through the glass, so the camera is valid.
- **All other views** (front_corner, aerial, top, rear_side, facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, porte_cochere, lobby_tower, wing_contact, parapet_corner, roof_plant, rear_entry): the target is unoccluded and unclipped.
