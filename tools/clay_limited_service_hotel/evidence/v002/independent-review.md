# Independent holistic review — hotel v002 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). All 18 renders, the 4 phone boards and the 3 locked sources were opened at full resolution; enlarged crops and pixel samples were taken where the finding depends on a few pixels. Only rendered pixels were judged against locked source pixels.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the massing, storey count, lobby tower, two porte-cocheres, low wing and roof plant are all present and the openings have real reveals, but the two 4-storey legs of the L do not join (daylight passes through the junction on both side elevations), the stone and charcoal material hierarchy that defines the source is absent, and the low wing, parking court and lobby atrium each deviate from the locked sources in a clearly visible way.
