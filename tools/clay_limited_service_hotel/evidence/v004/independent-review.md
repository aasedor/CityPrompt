# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: limited-service hotel, v004

I judged this only from the pixels of `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 renders at full resolution (1440x1080) and the four phone boards. I also read zoomed crops of `top.png`, `rear_entry.png` and `front_corner.png`, and sampled the pixel values of the dark ground patches.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the building envelope, storey count, L-plan, lobby tower, both porte-cocheres and the low wing now match the sources, and every v003 building finding is fixed. Five P1 defects still block approval: pure-black holes in the ground, the missing stone ground storey and stone piers, the missing drive loop, floating cars parked across the stalls, and a front camera that does not match the source front.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 7**
