# Independent holistic review: clay-vaulted-lecture-hall v003 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. I judged rendered pixels against the three locked sources only. I did not use metadata, counts or builder notes as proof. I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, the three sources and the four phone boards. My zoomed crops are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-v003-crops/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: three v002 findings are fixed in the pixels (end-arch rims, soffit rib grid, fin return), but six P1 defects remain. Two of them are carried over from v002: the pinwheel is only partly fixed and the white mass is still a box. The four new ones are a pure-black seam, warped and clashing louvre faces, missing louvred faces in the front view, and oblique cameras from the wrong quadrant. The candidate cannot be approved at production quality.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 6, P2 = 7

## 4. Camera validity

- **front_corner, aerial:** The target is not occluded or clipped, but both are from the wrong quadrant (SE instead of the source oblique's SW). The building fills only about 30% of the frame width, so the fins, slots and masses are close to illegible on the phone board. The matched oblique comparison cannot be made (P1 finding #6).
- **front:** Valid. It is a near-frontal south view, slightly more frontal than the source's slight-SW three-quarter view. The trees overlap the fins but do not hide any identity element.
- **top:** Valid. It is true plan, south down and west left, matching the source top's orientation.
- **left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side:** Valid, legible, not clipped.
- **Detail views** (facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, y_columns, fin_screen, vault_ribs, roof_masses, entrance_steps, interior, rear_north): each has its target legible and unclipped. In y_columns the tree canopy covers the upper-left third, but the Y-column at the right is clear. In entrance_steps the tree trunk is centred and the steps stay legible.
