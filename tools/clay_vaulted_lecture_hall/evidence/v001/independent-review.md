# Independent holistic review: clay-vaulted-lecture-hall v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. Evidence used: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution, the 4 `boards/*.png`, and zoomed crops written only to `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-lect-v001-crops/`. Builder metadata, counts, hashes and notes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the interior camera is completely black and the rooftop masses are missing the pinwheel layout. On top of that, the vault reads as a semicircular greenhouse barrel with closed glass ends instead of the source's broad open-ended cushion vault, and the "sculpted" white mass is a plain box.

Counts: P0 = 2, P1 = 8, P2 = 7
