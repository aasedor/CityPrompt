## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the bridge's identity reads correctly: twin inclined silver-grey tied arches, cross ties, hangers, a red cycle lane and a pale footway split by a raised grass planter, steel railings and brick quays. However, three clearly visible fidelity defects block approval: the plan is square to the canal instead of skewed, there are 5 cross ties instead of 7, and the deck edge is too heavy and sits too close to the water.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 7

## 4. Camera validity

- All 18 render views are valid. In none of them is the target occluded so much that the assessed element cannot be read, none clips the asset so that the element under review is lost, and none is illegible.
- front: the arches are cropped by the left and right frame edges. This is an expected consequence of an on-deck station and the deck, planter and crown ties are legible. The station does not match the source front (P2 #6).
- aerial: the foreground context parapet partly occludes the east springing (P2 #9). The rest of the bridge is fully legible.
- left_side and right_side: legible. The bridge is small in frame between context blocks (P2 #9).
- quay_under and deck_edge: low and dark, but the soffit, fascia, quay wall and water line are legible. These two views are what reveal #3.
- front_corner, aerial, quay_under: the scene boundary is visible (P2 #5). This does not invalidate any view.
