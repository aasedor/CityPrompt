## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The overall massing, the 14 x 13 bay counts, the storey count, the cantilever, the penthouse position, the roof plant and the site plan all match. Three clearly visible façade-section and proportion defects stop it from reading as the source's precast "egg-crate" box:

- the parapet is not a plain band;
- the fins are full-height pilasters instead of tapered fins within a cellular grid;
- the recessed ground storey is too short.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 8
