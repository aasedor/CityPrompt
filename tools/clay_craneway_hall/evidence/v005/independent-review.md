# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: clay crane-way hall v005

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Evidence used: only `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution (plus enlarged crops), and the 4 `boards/*.png`. No build code, reports or builder notes were read.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** Both v004 P1s are fixed in the pixels. Three P1 fidelity defects remain: the roof monitors are turned 90 degrees, the long elevations lack the source's railed gallery and outer glazed upper band (a broad blank lean-to roof sits there instead), and the nave gable has no glazed band under the triangle.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 9**

## 4. Camera validity

- No view is occluded or clipped, and none is illegible. All 18 renders show their target.
- `front` is valid but weak: small, distant and square-on, and it does not match the source front's 3/4 role (P2 #9).
- `top` is a nadir plan where the source is a high oblique (P2 #9).
- `stair_contact` crops the lower stair foot at the bottom edge (about 620-720, 1040-1080). Ground contact of the foot is confirmed in `front` (about 300, 645) instead.
- `interior` and `crane_interior` are legible.
