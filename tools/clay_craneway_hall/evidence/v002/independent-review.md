# Independent holistic review — crane-way hall, candidate v002 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent, no relationship with the builder. Judged only rendered pixels (18 renders at 1440x1080, 4 phone boards) against the three locked sources (`sources/front.png` 1024x768, `sources/oblique.jpg` 1195x896, `sources/top.jpg` 1195x896). Builder notes, counts, hashes and evidence JSON were not used as proof. Zoomed crops were cut to a temporary folder outside the candidate directory; no candidate file was modified.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the hall reads as the right family (stepped nave-and-aisle, clerestory, three monitor rows, open gable mouth with green sliding doors, lattice aisle walls, external stair, sidings) but the roof section is inverted against all three sources (outer monitor rows sit on the aisle roofs below the clerestory instead of on the nave roof above it), one detail camera shows nothing of its target, and five clearly visible fidelity defects remain on the primary faces.

- `front`, `front_corner`, `aerial`, `top` (true nadir, all 11 monitors and both sidings legible), `left_side`, `right_side`, `rear_side` — valid, nothing clipped.
