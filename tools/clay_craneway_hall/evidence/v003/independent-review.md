# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — crane-way hall clay, v003

Reviewer: independent (did not build v002 or v003). Evidence: the three locked sources, all 18 renders and the four phone boards, opened at full resolution. Builder notes, counts and hashes were not used as proof. Where a judgement needed numbers, pixel luminance was sampled from the PNGs; nothing was written or run other than this file.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — v003 fixes one of the two v002 P0s (monitor rows are now on the nave roof) and three of the five v002 P1s (switchback stair, interior camera inside the hall, no black bars at monitor corners), but the `side_doors` camera still does not show its target, the gable-mouth open frame is still nave-only against two sources showing it full-width, and the lattice columns are still roughly half the source's proportion.
