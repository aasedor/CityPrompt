# Independent holistic review — school/v003 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewed from rendered pixels only: all 18 `renders/*.png` at 1440x1080, the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`) and the four phone boards, each opened at full resolution. Builder notes, counts, hashes and the evidence JSONs were not used as proof. Every suspected seam or band was re-read as raw RGB samples (read-only) before it was recorded; "pure black" below means sampled values of (0–8, 0–5, 0–3), which no lit or shaded surface in these renders reaches (the darkest shaded brick return samples at ≈(25,17,9), the soffits at ≈(52–59)).

The v002 defects were re-verified in the pixels, not from the builder's word; their status is tabulated at the end of section 2.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the U, playground, fins, corten storey, rails, fence/gate and street-line entrance block now read as the locked school and both v002 wing-junction slots are closed, but a pure-black void runs from pavement to parapet at the entrance block's west junction (the passage's east jamb) in seven views, so the envelope is still not closed at the building's primary identity element.
