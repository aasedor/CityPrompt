# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — school v004 (clay-courtyard-primary-school)

Reviewer: independent, no relationship to the builder. Evidence base: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; the 18 renders in `renders/`; the 4 boards in `boards/`. All opened at full resolution. Pixel coordinates below are in the 1440x1080 render frame (sources in their native frame). Pure-black means RGB max <= 4; no legitimately lit clay surface in these renders reaches that value (the darkest shadowed interior under the canopy samples at ~12-25), so pure-black regions are read as unlit back-faces or open mesh, i.e. voids. Working crops used for the inspection are in `scratchpad/review-crops-school-v004/`; nothing in the candidate directory other than this file was touched.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the v003 vertical black void at the entrance-block / canopy junction is still a pure-black slot in this version's pixels (it has been boxed in a window frame, not closed), and a second pure-black void runs the full height of the entrance recess reveal in seven views; the massing, U-plan, playground, fins, corten storey, rails and fence otherwise conform.

## 4. Camera validity

All 18 cameras are valid: in every view the named target is inside the frame, unoccluded and legible, and no part of the asset is clipped by the frame except by design in the three tight details (glass_close, interior, fins_close), which are framed on their subject. left_side and rear show blank party walls, which is the correct content for those faces. top is a true nadir with the site slab and cast shadow visible; aerial and front_corner both show the full envelope. The "interior" view is shot from the courtyard through the classroom glazing rather than from inside; the interior (tables, ceiling lights) is legible through the glass, so the view is accepted as valid but should be labelled as a glazing/interior-through-glass view. No view has nothing legible or a target hidden behind another element.
