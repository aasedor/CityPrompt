# Independent holistic review — school/v002 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewed from rendered pixels only: all 18 `renders/*.png` at 1440x1080, the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`) and the four phone boards. Builder notes, counts and hashes were not used as evidence. Suspected seams were re-read as enlarged pixel crops before being recorded.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the massing, storey count, fins, corten storey, rails, fence/gate and playground all read as the locked school, but the U is built from four unjoined boxes with open through-slots at both wing junctions and the entrance passage column does not meet its slab, so the envelope and contact gates fail.
