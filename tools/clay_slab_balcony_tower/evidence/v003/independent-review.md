# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — slab tower candidate v003

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Inputs used: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 renders in `renders/`; the four phone boards. Every render and every source was opened at full resolution; enlarged working crops and pixel samples were made under `scratchpad/review-crops-slab-v003/` and are referenced by name below. Builder notes, counts, hashes and evidence JSON were not used as proof of anything.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — no P0, but four clearly visible P1 defects remain (a pure-black band on the entrance canopy fascia, black unassigned caps still present at parapet corners, fin stubs standing proud of the parapet in every aerial view, and a rear roller door split by a fin), so the candidate cannot be approved at production quality.

### v002 P1 verification (in this version's pixels)
- Brick parapet band capping the fins: **resolved** — parapet coping is pale concrete in `front_parapet`, `left_parapet`, `right_parapet`, `aerial_roofedge`; the only brick at the roofline is the top-storey spandrel under the roof slab, which the oblique source also shows.
- Black caps at the parapet corners: **not resolved** — see finding 2 (black faces persist on the side-facing planes of at least two corners and at the roof_penthouse corner).
- Brick spandrel strips hanging below the ground-floor soffit: **substantially resolved** — reduced to a hairline on the soffit (finding 5, P2).
- Canopy on rods attaching to nothing: **resolved as a support problem** (the canopy now bears on a column in fin_contact and lobby_entry and reaches the lobby glazing line) but **replaced by a new finish defect** (finding 1: pure-black fascia band).

## 4. Camera validity

- All 18 views have a legible target; no asset is clipped and nothing is occluded to the point of illegibility.
- `base_corner`: the foreground tree occludes most of the entrance canopy and lobby glazing behind it (tree crown at ~750–1200 × 300–650). The named target (the corner fin and base) is fully visible, so the camera is valid; the canopy is judged from `fin_contact`, `lobby_entry` and `front` instead.
- `interior`: valid but underexposed (finding 10).
- `rear_lane`: the shadow line between the first-floor slab soffit and the brick wall top (luminance 24–39, graded, not pure black) is a shadowed recess, not an open slot — checked by column sampling at x = 100, 300, 500, 800, 900, 1200.
