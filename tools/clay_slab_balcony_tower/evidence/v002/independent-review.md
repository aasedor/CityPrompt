**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the v001 blockers are genuinely fixed in the pixels, but the roof edge now reads as a brick parapet band capping truncated fins, the four parapet corners carry black unassigned caps, brick spandrel volumes hang below the slab soffits, and the entrance canopy hangs on rods that attach to nothing; 0 P0, 4 P1, 9 P2.

### v001 P1 verification (pixels only)

- Centre-bay spandrel/window ratio inverted → **fixed.** front.png column x=720: per 57-58 px storey the glass run is 44-46 px and the brick run 12-14 px (window ~78 %, brick ~22 %, brick under the window, slab line below). Source right-hand crop reads window ~68 %, brick ~18 %, slab ~14 %.
- Centre bay too wide → **fixed.** front.png row y=540: outer bay 509-649 (141 px), centre 657-782 (126 px incl. brick jambs), outer 790-930 (141 px), with ~6 px fins and ~30 px corner fins; centre ≈ 27.5 % of the face vs ≈ 28 % in the top source (left edge: 400 / 290 / 360 px in the 2x plan crop).
- Fin stubs above the parapet → **removed, but over-corrected** into finding 1 (fins now stop below a brick band instead of reaching a concrete coping).
- Black ground-floor soffit → **fixed** (soffit mid-grey in lobby_entry, fin_contact); residual dark glazing is finding 9.
