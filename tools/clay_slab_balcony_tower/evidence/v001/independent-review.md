# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — clay-slab-balcony-tower v001

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Evidence: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 `renders/*.png`; `boards/phone-01..04`. All opened at full resolution before writing. Builder notes, counts and hashes were ignored as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the massing, storey count, bay rhythm and every identity element are present and topologically sound (0 × P0), but four clearly visible fidelity/optical defects (brick-dominant centre bay from an inverted spandrel-to-window ratio and an over-wide centre bay, fin stubs breaking the parapet silhouette, and a pure-black ground-floor soffit that reads as a void) block production-quality approval (4 × P1, 9 × P2).
