Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence: only `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` opened at full resolution, and the 4 `boards/*.png`. Zoomed crops were written only to `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/ring-review-crops-479026274/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the bridge's identity is correct (red ring deck, white railings, single central pylon, radial cable fan, four columns, one straight ramp, intersection beneath), but no render shows where the ramp meets the ground. There are also five clearly visible fidelity or camera defects.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 5, P2 = 5

## 4. Camera validity

- **ramp_landing**: invalid for its stated target. The landing is outside the frame (#1, P0).
- **front**: legible, but not a valid matched-role counterpart to the source front. The camera is on the deck; the near arc, near column and ramp are all absent (#4, P1).
- **front_corner, aerial, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side, rear_north**: the asset is clipped, because the pylon tip leaves the top edge. In front_corner the cable collar itself is cut (#5, P1). The ramp is also cut at the frame edge in aerial, front_corner, left_side and right_side (#1).
- **pylon_head**: the mast above the collar is clipped at the top edge (#5).
- **rear_side**: the foreground context roof covers the lower-left fifth but does not cover the bridge. Valid.
- **facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, cable_fan, ring_deck, column_support, intersection_under, rear_north, top**: target legible and not covered. Valid. (glass_close is valid as a camera, but has no glass to show; see #3.)
