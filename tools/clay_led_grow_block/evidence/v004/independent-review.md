# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: farm / clay-led-grow-block v004

**Verdict: VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** Envelope, storeys, roof programme and contacts now hold, but three P1 defects remain: the magenta rack light renders as near-white pink, the south-face ground storey is a concrete band where the sources show charcoal cladding, and the perimeter rail is open at every corner.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 8**

Method: I opened all three locked sources, all 18 renders and all 4 phone boards at full resolution. I made zoom crops of the source facades, the render plinths, the roof corners and the rail junctions, and I measured magenta saturation with an HSV mask (hue 0.77 to 0.95, V > 0.5). Faces were oriented from the roof plan: penthouse NW, beds NE, glasshouses S. Matched roles: `oblique.jpg` and `aerial.png` are both taken from the south-east. `front_corner.png` is taken from the south-west. `front.png` is a straight south elevation. In both `front.png` and `oblique.jpg`, the south face is the left face and the east face is the right face.

Note on the brief: the brief text says the entrance is "near the south-west corner". Both locked sources show it on the **east** face, near the **south-east** corner: `front.png` right face x 655-720, y 550-660, and `oblique.jpg` right face near x 680-730, y 740-800. I judged against the source pixels.

Note that `front_corner` is taken from the south-west, so it does not match the source oblique, which is taken from the south-east. `aerial` does match the oblique role. This is acceptable, but a south-east corner street view matching `front.png` would make the source comparison direct.
