## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — every v002 envelope defect is repaired, but the roof programme is rotated 90° relative to the plinth entrance (the entrance sits under the glasshouse gable ends, whereas both sources put it on the face that runs along the glasshouse flanks), and the magenta lit depth that was restored in the elevations still vanishes in every oblique and aerial view.

## 4. Camera validity

All 18 views are valid: the target is visible, unoccluded and legible in each, and no view shows the asset clipped by a near plane or rendered empty.

- `interior` is a slot close-up (three slots on one face at the face normal), not a glasshouse or grow-floor interior; it is legible and is the clearest evidence that the racks and light exist behind every slot.
- `penthouse_contact` frames the blank west wall of the penthouse; contact with the deck, coping and rail is legible, but the view says nothing about the penthouse's door/plant face — a re-render should frame the long face.
- `roof_greenhouses` now keeps all five houses and the east rail inside the frame (the v002 clipping is gone).
- `beds` keeps all four beds and the corner rail inside the frame.
- `front_corner` and `aerial` are the matched-role views for `sources/oblique.jpg`; they are valid cameras, and they are exactly where findings 1 and 2 are visible.
