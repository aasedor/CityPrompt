# Independent holistic review — farm/v002 (clay-led-grow-block)

Reviewer opened all 18 renders, all 4 phone boards and the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`) at full resolution, plus pixel crops of the east parapet in `right_side`, `top`, `aerial`, `front_corner` and `roof_greenhouses`. Judgement is on rendered pixels versus locked source pixels only.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the massing, storey count, slot cadence and roof programme are right, but the building's defining magenta-lit slot depth is missing from four of the six bays on every face (zero magenta pixels in all three oblique envelope views), and the easternmost glasshouse hangs through the east parapet and rail.

## 4. Camera validity

All 18 views are valid: the target is visible, unoccluded and legible in each.

- `roof_greenhouses`: the easternmost glasshouse is cut by the right frame edge; the row is still legible. Not invalid, but the clipped edge is exactly where finding 2 lives, so the re-render should frame it.
- `beds`: the northernmost bed is cut by the left frame edge; the four-bed group and rail contact remain legible.
- `interior`: this view is a slot close-up (three slots on one face), not a glasshouse or grow-floor interior; it is legible and is the clearest evidence for findings 1 and 6.
- No view has the target occluded, nothing is rendered empty, and no view shows the asset clipped by a near plane.
