# Independent holistic review: clay-steel-truss-road-bridge v001 (RLASM v6.1, architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent; I did not build this candidate. Evidence is rendered pixels only, compared with `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I opened all 18 renders, the 4 phone boards and the 3 sources at full resolution. I made zoomed crops only in a new scratch folder (`trussbr-review-crops-457629310`). I ignored build metadata, counts and builder notes.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the bridge's identity is mostly right. It is a rust-red Pratt through-truss with inclined end posts, lattice top struts and portals over a two-lane road, with outside sidewalks and railings, brick abutments and river quays. But the whole-envelope `front` camera is invalid, and four visible fidelity/coherence defects block approval: the truss web rhythm, the top-lateral rhythm, a raised concrete bar across the carriageway at both ends, and an occluded `rear` view.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 4, P2 = 10

## 4. Camera validity

- **front: INVALID (P0, #1).** The asset is clipped at the top. The end post, portal, abutment and river are out of frame, so the view cannot be compared with the front source.
- **rear: partly occluded (P1, #5).** The bus massing hides most of the right truss and its foot.
- **front_corner:** valid. The near-left foot touches the bottom edge (P2 #13).
- **river_underside:** valid but underexposed. The detail it should show (abutment seats, bearings) is not legible (P2 #12).
- **aerial, top, left_side, right_side, rear_side:** valid. The whole envelope is visible and legible.
- **facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, portal_frame, truss_panel, deck_sidewalk, abutment, roadway_inside, rear_north:** valid detail views. Each target is legible and unobstructed. `glass_close` frames a top-chord gusset node, since a bridge has no glazing.
