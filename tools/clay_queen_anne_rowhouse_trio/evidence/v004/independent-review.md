# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: Queen Anne rowhouse trio, v004

Reviewer: independent (no build code, reports or builder notes read). Evidence: sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg, sources/top.jpg, all 18 renders at 1440x1080 (with pixel crops), and boards phone-01 to phone-04.
Pixel coordinates refer to the full-resolution render named.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the massing, storey count, three-unit rhythm, chimneys, fence and rear gables now read correctly, but seven P1 defects remain. They are in the eaves/gable junction (pent band, bargeboard feet, open apexes), on both end walls, and in the identity detail (banded brick, gable infill and arched windows, two-storey canted bays).

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 7, P2 = 12**
