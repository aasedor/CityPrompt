# Independent holistic review — rowhouse v002 (RLASM v6.1 clay candidate)

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Inputs opened at full resolution with the Read tool: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, and all 18 `renders/*.png`. Zoomed crops of the front facade, dormer, stoops, roof plan and east wall were made in a separate scratch folder to confirm what the full-resolution pixels show; nothing in the candidate directory other than this file was touched. The builder's notes, counts and hashes were not used as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the terrace identity (three units, two canted bays under bargeboard gables, central dormer, slate roof, iron fence) is present, but the dormer is topologically broken (its window frame floats above the gable apex and its brick cheeks punch through the roof), the front rhythm swaps a source window for a second door and drops two first-floor windows, the gable windows are painted outlines with battens running through them, the stoops are black blocks with no treads, and both end walls carry a floating eaves slab plus a black void.
