# Independent holistic review: clay-stone-arch-road-bridge v001 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. I judged rendered pixels only, against the three locked sources (sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg, sources/top.jpg). I opened all 18 renders at full resolution and all 4 phone boards. I did not use builder metadata, counts, hashes or notes as evidence. Zoomed crops used for confirmation are in a new folder at /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/stonebr-review-crops-19727478.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the core bridge grammar is present (three semicircular voussoired arches, pointed cutwaters, dentil cornice, balustrades with pedestals, double lamps), but the governing front view clips a third of the bridge and its near approach (P0), and ten P1 defects block production approval: plan topology, approaches, contact gaps, lamps, scene edges, palette and cameras.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 10, P2 = 8

## 4. Camera validity

- **front: invalid (P0, F1).** The whole-envelope view clips the asset: second pier, third arch and near abutment and approach are out of frame. It is not a matched role for front.jpg.
- **front_corner: partially clipped (P1, F2).** The near abutment and approach are cut by the bottom edge; all three arches are visible.
- **rear_side: occluded (P1, F10).** Two foreground trees hide the near arch, pier and abutment.
- **left_side / right_side: target illegible (P1, F11).** The bridge is under 8% of frame height, no structure is readable, and the two views duplicate each other.
- **roadway_deck: partially occluded (P2, F18).** The bus hides the far deck; the carriageway and footways are still legible.
- **glass_close:** a building role name reused for a lamp close-up. The target is legible and it duplicates lamp_standard; acceptable, but it adds no coverage.
- **Valid:** aerial, top, rear, rear_north, facade_close, architecture_close, arch_spandrel, cutwater_pier, balustrade, lamp_standard and river_underside. Their targets are framed, unoccluded and legible. Scene-edge artefacts visible in several of them are logged under F8, not as camera faults.
