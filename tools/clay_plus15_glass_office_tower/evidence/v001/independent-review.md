# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — plus15 / v001 (clay)

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Evidence: the 18 renders in `renders/`, the three locked sources in `sources/`, and the four phone boards in `boards/`, every one opened at full resolution. Builder notes, counts and hashes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the `interior` camera returns a frame with nothing legible (P0), and five clearly visible P1 defects (an unlit black slot between the office stack and the lobby colonnade, voids at every mullion/spandrel junction, a Plus 15 bridge that ends in mid-air over the carriageway, a penthouse that is a near-solid box with its L rotated 180° from the sources, and see-through hollow floor plates in the close glass views) block production-quality approval, even though the tower's silhouette, sixteen-storey rhythm, corner columns, colonnade and green roof are otherwise well matched.
