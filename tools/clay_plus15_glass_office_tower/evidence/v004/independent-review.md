# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: plus15 v004

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 7. The tower shaft, the facade grammar, the bridge and the three-sided roof layout now read correctly. Three things still block approval: the base is squashed to about 1.55 storeys where the sources show a tall double-height colonnade; the mechanical enclosure is a low picket rail, not the full-height glazed and louvred penthouse; and the green roof is reduced to thin raised planter strips.

Method: I opened the three locked sources, all 18 renders at full resolution, the four phone boards and zoomed crops of the lobby, roof and corners. I read no build code, reports or notes. Pixel coordinates refer to the 1440x1080 renders unless a source is named.
