# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — clay-plus15-glass-office-tower v002

Reviewer: independent (did not build v001 or v002). Evidence: pixels of `renders/*.png` (18), `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, `boards/phone-0[1-4]*.png`, all opened at full resolution. Builder notes, counts, hashes and evidence JSON were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — zero P0 (the v001 P0 black interior is now legible, the bridge lands, the spandrel/beam slot and junction squares are gone), but five P1 defects remain: the mechanical penthouse is less than half the height the oblique shows and still reads as a solid white box without louvre fins, the rooftop units have been moved onto the penthouse roof instead of the lower deck the sources show, the lower-floor vision glass is still see-through over empty floor plates, and the interior view is crushed to black above and below the glazing strip.
