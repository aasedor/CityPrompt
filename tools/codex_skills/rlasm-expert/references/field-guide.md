# RLASM v6 field guide

## Authority

Use exact locked pixels first, then `docs/RLASM_LATEST_METHOD.md`, then
`tools/archetype_compiler/rlasm_method.json`. Candidate records and current
holistic review follow. Historical package-local recipes are evidence of how a
candidate was made, not current instructions.

## Phase gates

| Phase | Required proof | Hard stop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source lock | exact IDs, compatible front/oblique/top plus available views, bytes and SHA-256 | mixed sibling or missing hash |
| Measurement | ratios, plan/roof graph, bay schedule, program, asymmetry, camera roster | family label substituted for exact building |
| Unskinned envelope | closed whole building, family-specific constructors, load paths, circulation | hero-only facade, generic rear, empty transparent shell |
| Identity/material | bounded orthographic identity, exact-family specimens, metre-scale axes, one owner | perspective crop, renamed generic shader, duplicate pixels |
| Opening/contact | cut carrier, returns, recessed optical assembly, occupied depth, seated projections | card over solid wall, floating dormer, open valley |
| Evidence | complete neutral full-res cameras, signature views, 1080×1920 phone board | cropped/blank/dark evidence |
| Approval | separate holistic adversarial review, zero P0/P1 | builder or scoped self-promotion |

## Family constructors

- Civic tower: occupied setbacks, continuous pier/window/spandrel grammar,
  restrained entrance and relief hierarchy.
- Log lodge: asymmetric plan and gables, coursed logs cut at openings, corner
  ends, coherent closed valleys, continuous fieldstone support.
- Siheyuan: axial compound, traversable moon gate, differentiated halls,
  post-between-opening schedule, lifted ceramic eaves and planted court.
- Train station: enclosed glass-and-iron shed, monumental end frames, low
  headhouse/tower hierarchy, tracks, platforms, canopies, concourse and stairs.
- Second Empire civic building: continuous warm masonry authority, constructed
  mansard, integrated dormer cheeks, seated caps/flashing, cupola load path.

## Material decisions

Matching colour is insufficient. Match bond/course morphology, grain or tile
direction, joint scale, finish, roughness, oxidation, weathering, and physical
scale. Map wall fields with front/rear X-Z and side Y-Z authority; map slopes in
slope-local coordinates. Same-substance trim may vary in dressing and relief,
but not drift into a second hue family.

## Evidence discipline

Baseline: front, front-corner, aerial, left, right, rear, rear-side, facade
close, architecture close, and glass close. Add the fragile relationship:
dormer contact, gate axis, platform oblique, track axis, courtyard, roof
junction, or signature detail. Whole views include grade to highest object and
complete visible footprint with margin.
