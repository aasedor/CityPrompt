# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — clay-brick-podium-condo-tower v001

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Evidence: the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), all 18 renders in `renders/` and the four phone boards in `boards/`, each opened at full resolution plus zoom crops of the podium/tower junction, the balcony stacks, the terrace and the corner column. Build report, manifest, hashes and builder notes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the tower/podium datum is wrong (the curtain wall floats ~1.2 m above the terrace deck with an open void beneath it and every stack's lowest balcony hangs below the podium cornice on blank brick), and the `interior` camera renders solid black, so the candidate cannot pass with zero P0/P1.
