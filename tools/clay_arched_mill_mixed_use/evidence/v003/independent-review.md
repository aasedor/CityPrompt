# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — clay-arched-mill-mixed-use v003

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence used: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png`, the four `boards/*.png`. Builder notes, counts, hashes and evidence JSON were not used as proof. Working crops are in `scratchpad/review-crops-mill-v003/` (not part of the candidate).

Face vocabulary used below (the brief's compass labels do not match the sources consistently, so faces are named by what the sources show):

- **Gable face (G)** — the raked-parapet face with the chimney rising behind its far end. Source: right-hand face in `front.png` and `oblique.jpg`. Render: `front.png`.
- **Pavilion face (A)** — the eave face whose roof slope carries the glazed pavilion and terrace rail. Source: left-hand face in `front.png` and `oblique.jpg`; left edge of `top.jpg`. Render: `left_side.png`.
- **Rear eave face (R)** — opposite A, not visible in any source. Render: `right_side.png`, `rear_yard.png`.
- **Far gable (D)** — opposite G, not visible in any source. Render: `rear.png`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the four-storey, four-bay, arched, pilastered, slate-roofed mill with pavilion and chimney is recognisably there and the v002 bay-count P0 is fixed, but the rear chimney breast interpenetrates four sill plates (contact P0), and the gable face, pavilion massing and chimney position still deviate visibly from the locked sources (four P1).
