Reviewer: independent holistic reviewer. Evidence used: only `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution (with zoomed crops of the junctions in question), and the 4 `boards/*.png`. ## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: on both eave (long) faces the top storey has lost its segmental arches. Those eight openings are flat-headed and their heads run up into the dentil course, while the sources show arched top-storey windows with brick above them under the cornice.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 1, P2 = 7**
