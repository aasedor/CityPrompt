# Independent holistic review: clay-regency-stucco-terrace v005 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent (not the builder). Evidence used: only `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` (each opened at full resolution) and the four `boards/*.png`. I did not use builder notes, metadata or hashes as proof. My zoomed crops are in a new folder, `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/regency-v005-indep-crops-86524805/`.

Orientation check from the pixels: the brick terrace is on the north side (top of `top.png`, far left of `front.png`, back right of `front_corner.png`). The south face has the central pediment and one porch. The west and east faces each have three porches and three fin and stack pairs. In the locked oblique, the pedimented south face is on the left, the three-porch east face is on the right and the brick terraces are behind it. The locked top has the pediment roof at the centre of the south edge and two fins on each of the west and east edges, plus a fin at each north corner. The render therefore follows the source plan. The brief's own wording puts the porches on the "long south front" and the pediments on the "east and west ends". That wording does not match the locked pixels, and I judged against the pixels.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the façades, giant order, porches and front camera now hold, but the roof still does not read as the source roof. The render roof is mostly pale ribbed lead with thin slate strips, where the source roof is mostly slate with narrow lead bands. The inner court dormers are also still ordinary small dormers on a thin steep strip, where the source has large box dormers on broad inner slate slopes.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 2, P2 = 11
