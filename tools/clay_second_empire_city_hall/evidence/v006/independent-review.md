# Independent holistic review: clay-second-empire-city-hall v006 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent; I did not build this candidate. Only rendered pixels and the locked source pixels were used as evidence: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full 1440x1080 resolution, and the 4 phone boards. I opened full-resolution crops to inspect contact and depth, and ran a scan for pure-black pixels (all channels < 22) to locate voids. I did not use metadata, build reports, hashes or the builder's notes as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the attic storey, the quoin sizing, the forecourt and lawn strips, and the continuous dormer hood band are now fixed in the pixels, but three P1 defects carried over from v005 are still visible in reduced form: tower side windows cut by the main roof, dormer hood ends with black wedges and a segmented voussoir ring, and pure-black contact voids.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 11

## 4. Camera validity

No view is invalid. In every view the target is unoccluded and unclipped, and the subject is legible:

- front, front_corner and aerial frame the building small, at about 40-50 % of the width (P2 #13).
- left_side has two trees partly in front of two bays. This is minor and does not hide any identity element.
- interior and rear_court are legible.
- clock_tower shows the whole tower, from the stone stage to the lantern finial.
