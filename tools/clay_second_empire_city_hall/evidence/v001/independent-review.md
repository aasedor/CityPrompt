# Independent holistic review: clay-second-empire-city-hall v001

Reviewer: independent RLASM v6.1 holistic reviewer (not the builder). Evidence used: only the pixels of `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution, and the four `boards/phone-*.png`. I ignored metadata, hashes, counts and builder notes.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**. The massing reads as the right building type (a three-storey Second Empire block around a court, with a central clock tower, four corner pavilions, a grand stair and a fenced lawn forecourt), but it has 13 P1 defects. They fall into three groups:
- contact and geometry faults visible in every whole-envelope view: open black seams, open dormer cheeks and black pediment tympana;
- missing or misplaced identity elements: the porch pediment, the tower's placement and cap, and the tall pavilion mansards;
- source-rhythm, roof-form, material and landscape deviations.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 13, P2 = 9

## 4. Camera validity

- **rear_court:** invalid for its role. It does not show the inner court, only an outer elevation, so the court is never seen at detail scale (P1, finding 13).
- **Whole-envelope views (front, left_side, right_side, rear, aerial, front_corner, rear_side, top):** no clipping and no occlusion of the asset. All are legible but under-framed: the building fills about 35–50% of the frame width, and phone-board legibility is marginal (P2, finding 19).
- **Detail views (facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, clock_tower, porch_stair, pavilion_mansard, cornice_contact, roof_court):** targets are in frame and legible. clock_tower shows the full tower from the arched stage to the lantern. porch_stair shows the full stair and porch.
- **interior:** legible, no clipping.
