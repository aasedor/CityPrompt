# Independent holistic review: clay-second-empire-city-hall v004 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent; I did not build this candidate. Evidence: I opened sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg and sources/top.jpg, all 18 renders at full resolution, and the four phone boards. Where a judgement depended on fine relief, I also sampled pixel rows from the render and source PNGs, without writing any files. I did not use metadata, hashes or builder notes as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**. All four v003 P1 findings are fixed in the pixels. Three identity-level fidelity defects remain: the second, upper tier of iron balconies on the corner pavilions is missing; the rusticated stone base and quoins are absent; and every dormer has a flat box cap where the sources show round or segmental hoods.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 9
