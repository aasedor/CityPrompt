# Independent holistic review: clay-second-empire-city-hall v005 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: an independent reviewer with no part in the build. I judged rendered pixels against the locked source pixels only. I opened all three sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), all 18 renders and all 4 phone boards with the Read tool. I also read enlarged crops of the renders and sources, saved outside the candidate directory in the session scratchpad (`ch5crops/`). I did not read builder metadata, reports, hashes or notes, and did not use them as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VERDICT: VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**. All three v004 P1s are fixed in the pixels. However, the corner pavilions still lack the attic storey that both sources show, and the new quoins and dormer hoods introduce visible voids, collisions and open joints. The forecourt is about four times deeper than in the top source, and the tower-shaft side pediments are buried in the main mansard.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 6, P2 = 10

## 5. Previous-version (v004) findings: resolved in pixels?

1. **Only one iron balcony per corner-pavilion face. Resolved.** Every pavilion face now has two stacked iron balconies on consoles: one at the arched main floor and one at the pedimented upper floor. Seen in front, architecture_close, porch_stair, left_side, right_side, rear and rear_side.
2. **No rustication; beige basement. Resolved, but the fix introduced new defects.** The basement is now a grey stone band with horizontal rustication grooves (architecture_close lower right, facade_close bottom, porch_stair). The pavilion corners and edges now carry long-and-short quoins. Banding on the pavilion main floor is present but faint and stays wall-beige (P2 #11). The new quoins are oversized: they overlap the adjacent windows (P1 #3) and contain black voids at plinth level (P1 #2).
3. **Dormers were boxes with flat caps. Resolved in form, but new defects.** Every dormer now has a segmental arched head, a curved zinc roof and a segmental hood moulding, and it reads as arched from every distance (cornice_contact, pavilion_mansard, front). The hoods are built from separate straight blocks with open black joints and overshooting ends (P1 #4).
