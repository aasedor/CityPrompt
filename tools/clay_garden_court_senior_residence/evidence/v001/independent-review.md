Reviewer: independent (not the builder). I judged only rendered pixels against the locked sources `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, plus the 4 phone boards. I made zoomed crops only in a new folder, `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/review-crops-senior-v001/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the whole-envelope massing, the ring plan, the roofs and the garden room are faithful, but three of the ten detail cameras render black or nothing legible (P0). There are also five P1 defects: dormer bodies pierce the wing ridges, flat-headed doors replace the arched garden doors, the forecourt drive loop is missing, the court_view frame clips out the fountain and beds, and a tree blocks the loggia_arcade view.

Counts: P0 = 3, P1 = 5, P2 = 11
