## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the span, deck, path, railings, corten pods, column piers and river context are all present and coherent with no floating or contact (P0) defects, but six clearly visible P1 fidelity, program and landscape defects block approval. These are the front camera, the bed-to-path proportion, the pod plan shape, pod access and two planting defects.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 6, P2 = 8
