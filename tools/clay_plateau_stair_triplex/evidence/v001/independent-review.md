# Independent holistic review: clay-plateau-stair-triplex v001 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent; did not build this candidate. Evidence: the rendered pixels of all 18 views in `renders/`, the four phone boards in `boards/`, and the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`). The builder's metadata, counts, hashes and notes were not used as proof. Zoomed crops used for inspection are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-crops-triplex-v001-r1/`.

## 1. Verdict

**Verdict: VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The plex reads correctly as a two-storey grey Plateau corner plex over a half-basement. It has quoins, a two-level recessed porch, red upper and green lower doors, and a curling black stair. However, the canopy columns are plain posts with no lattice, the canopy bracket and cornice corner intersect and leave gaps, the raised parapet and cartouche read as separate stacked boxes, and three camera roles (left_side, the distant envelope views, glass_close) do not frame a legible target.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 7, P2 = 11
