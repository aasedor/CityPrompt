## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the matched front camera clips the asset and leaves the south-west ETFE corner out of frame (P0). Ten P1 defects also block approval. They cover the pitch/bowl plan relationship, the facade diagonal grammar, the blank backing behind the diagrid, the ETFE cushion form, edges and extent, roof radial legibility, open node voids, the floating aisle planks and their stubs, and the incomplete street frame.
