Reviewer: independent; I did not build this candidate. I judged only the rendered pixels against the locked source pixels (sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg, sources/top.jpg). I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, plus the 4 phone boards. I checked zoomed crops, written only to `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-v002-crops/`. ## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the plan, pitch orientation, ETFE extent, radial trusses and aisle steps are now fixed. But the front camera still clips the south-west corner (P0). The ETFE cushions are still faceted near-square pyramids on a flat band, the facade still reads as stacked tiers with the wrong diagonal pitch, black open joints remain, and the bowl reads hollow at the corners and under the roof (P1s).
