# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — clay-courtyard-mixed-use-midrise v001

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Judged only rendered pixels in `renders/*.png` and `boards/*.png` against the locked sources `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`. Build report, manifest, counts and hashes were not used as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the raised-courtyard street stair, the single most identifying element of this building, is built as a roofed tunnel capped by a black lid that reads as a hole from every elevated view, and one detail camera (`interior`) shows nothing legible; seven further P1 fidelity and coherence defects (swapped bays on the corner wing, wrong east-face material and program, shallow penthouse terraces, missing rear-bar courtyard balconies, under-scaled roofscape, balustrade posts piercing slabs, storefront on the party wall) block production approval.

Counts: **P0 = 2, P1 = 7, P2 = 6.**
