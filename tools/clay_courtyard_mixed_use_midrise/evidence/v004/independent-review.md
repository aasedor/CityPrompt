Reviewer inputs: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 renders in `renders/`; the four boards in `boards/`. Builder notes, counts and hashes were ignored; only rendered pixels against locked source pixels were judged.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the massing, storey count, U-plan, street stair, cedar balconies, retail base and continuous set-back top storey now match the sources, but the roof plane is cut by a black slot along both wing/rear-bar junctions (P0), the street-level stair view still shows a black band across the stair head (unresolved from v003, P1), and the wings' projecting penthouse roof plane that defines the source silhouette is absent (P1).
