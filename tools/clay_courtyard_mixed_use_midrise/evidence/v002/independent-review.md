# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — courtyard mixed-use mid-rise, clay candidate v002

Reviewer: independent (did not build v001 or v002). Basis: rendered pixels in `renders/*.png` (18 views) and `boards/*.png` (4) judged only against the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`). Builder notes, counts and hashes were not used as evidence. Every render, source and board was opened at full resolution; the stair head, rear bar, penthouse bases and courtyard were additionally inspected at 2–4x crops.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the street stair is still roofed over by the courtyard deck (the v001 P0 is not fixed), the rear bar is one full storey short of the locked sources, and two of the detail cameras (`balcony_close`, `cladding_contact`) frame a blank wall with no target legible.

Counts: **4 P0, 5 P1, 7 P2.**
