# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — courtyard mixed-use mid-rise, clay candidate v005

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 `renders/*.png` opened at full 1440x1080 plus enlarged crops; `boards/phone-01..04.png`. No build code, reports or builder notes were read; the builder's claims in the brief were treated as hypotheses to verify in pixels. Pixel coordinates below are in the native render frame (1440x1080) or the native source frame (front 1024x768, oblique 1195x896, top 1376x768).

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the roof-slot hole is closed and the stair head is lit, but the rear-bar floating roof slab is still absent, the wing roof terraces are a 1.5 m ledge under the eave instead of the source's deep set-back decks, the wing courtyard faces are dark ribbed metal where both sources show tan/cedar, and a pure-black crevice remains under the right wing's roof slab at the rear-bar junction.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 4, P2 = 8.**
