## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the arcade-and-shopfront elevation reads well against the front source, but the cobbled street reads as a planked deck, the track bed has no ballast, the imposts do not carry the arches, trees cut into the terrace facades, the train is parked hard against a blind end wall of a viaduct closed at both ends, and two matched-role cameras show the wrong side of the viaduct.
