# Independent holistic review: stepg v001 (crow-stepped canal house, RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent; no relationship with the builder. Judged only the rendered pixels against the locked source pixels (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`). I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, plus the 4 phone boards. I made zoomed crops only in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-stepg-v001-crops/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The model is a recognisable crow-stepped canal house with the right 5-step coped gable, 3-bay rhythm, stoop and east chimney. But it has one more three-bay storey than the locked front and oblique show, the whole-envelope `left_side` camera is completely blocked by the west context box, and many P1 fidelity and contact defects remain: salmon siding-like brick, coarse 6-pane sashes, cross-shaped anchors, a modern-looking door, an open void at the roof/gable junction, protruding gutter ends, floating rails and a black stoop cheek.

Counts: P0 = 2, P1 = 13, P2 = 13

### Note on the brief vs the sources

The brief's prose describes "three bays ... on each of three upper floors". The locked front and oblique both show the raised ground floor, then **two** three-bay upper floors under the eave, then the single gable window. Following the rule that the locked sources are the only truth, #1 is recorded as P0. If the orchestrator decides the brief's storey count overrides the source pixels, #1 should be re-graded. The verdict would not change: P0 #2 and the 13 P1s would still block.
