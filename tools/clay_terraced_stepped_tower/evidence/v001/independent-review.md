# Independent holistic review: clay-terraced-stepped-tower v001 (RLASM v6.1 clay)

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Evidence used: locked `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution; `boards/phone-01..04`. Zoomed crops were written only to `scratchpad/indep-review-terr-v001-crops/`. Builder metadata, counts and notes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The main massing is right: a dark slab, a seven-step south wing on the east, and a birch court on the west. Five P1 defects remain. The east raking element is the wrong kind of element. The court-facing wing elevation is missing its continuous balconies. The ground colonnade is missing. The birch court has the wrong landscape. The terraces are far sparser than the source.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 10

## 4. Camera validity

- No render is clipped, empty or illegible. All 18 frame their target.
- **aerial**: the target is legible, but the birch court is largely hidden by the wing (P2, F13). Court identity is carried by birch_court and top.
- **front**: valid and legible. It is a ground-level due-south elevation, not the high SSW oblique of source front, so role-matched comparison runs through front_corner (P2, F9).

- **dark_block, rear_north, glass_close**: close views are legible. Reveals read on the block windows.
- No camera defect rises to P0 or P1.
