# Independent holistic review: clay-stepped-terraced-plaza, v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. Evidence: the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), all 18 renders opened at full resolution, the four phone boards, and zoomed crops written only to `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/rev-v001-indep-crops-7f3a/`. No metadata, counts or builder notes were used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: The plan, the central handrailed stair, the terraces and the flanking houses read correctly as a Cycladic stair-plaza. However, one required detail camera is fully black, the bougainvillea mesh passes through a house wall into the interior, and the chapel and belfry openings are flat applied panels with no depth. There are also nine P1 fidelity and coherence defects.

Counts: P0 = 3, P1 = 9, P2 = 5
