# Independent holistic review: clay-prewar-apartment-tower v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent, no relationship with the builder. Judged only the rendered pixels (18 renders, 4 phone boards) against the locked sources `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I ignored metadata, hashes, the build report and the builder's evidence. Zoomed crops are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-prewar-v001-crops/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the massing, storey count, base, quoins, cornice and balustrade, and site grammar are right, but five clearly visible P1 defects block production-quality approval. They are an under-scaled rooftop silhouette, a wrong copper roof finish, open corner joinery, floating balcony railings, and a south-face composition that swaps the balcony stack and the stone bay.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 9

## 4. Camera validity

- No view is invalid. The target is present, unclipped and legible in all 18 renders.
- In limestone_base, a street tree occludes about 30% of the frame, but the rustication and windows are still readable (P2).
- front_corner and aerial are nearly duplicate, steeper and farther than the source oblique, so the matched-role comparison with the oblique is weaker than it should be (P2).
- interior is underexposed but readable (P2).
- Whole-envelope coverage (front, front_corner, aerial, top, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side) is complete. The north and west faces, which no source shows, were judged for coherence only and are coherent with the visible grammar, apart from the 2x2 rear windows (P2).
