# Independent holistic review: prewar apartment tower, v002 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent; not the builder. Evidence used: only the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), all 18 renders at full resolution, and the four phone boards. I made zoomed crops only in a new folder (`scratchpad/indrev-v002-crops-7f3a/`). I ignored the builder's notes as proof; every claim below was checked in the pixels.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: v002 fixes most of the v001 rooftop and balcony defects, but the south street face now has a full-height stone pier that cuts a whole column of windows in half. The SE-corner entrance group also sits mid-face, away from where both elevational sources put it. The black slit at the cornice/pedestal corner is still there, the catwalk reads as a black slab, and the entrance detail camera cuts off the marquee.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 12

## 4. Camera validity

- **entrance_marquee**: the target (marquee) is clipped. Only about a third of the canopy and one tie rod are in frame, and the door is half out at the left edge. This is invalid for its stated purpose (finding 5, P1).
- **limestone_base**: a tree canopy and trunk occlude about a third of the frame and the marquee is clipped at the right edge. The base itself is still legible (P2, folded into finding 5's fix).
- **rear_court**: legible, but there is no court in the model, so the view shows a plain north wall (P2).
- **front_corner / aerial**: legible but small in frame (P2).
- **front, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side, top, facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, fire_balconies, cornice_balustrade, tank_house, interior**: valid. Target unoccluded, nothing clipped, legible.

## 5. Status of the v001 findings (verified in pixels)

1. **Tank house too low (about 1.2 storeys), chimneys barely clearing the balustrade**: **resolved (residual P2).**
   - Tank house: about 2.4 storeys above deck (front: eave y≈172 vs deck ≈295 at a 50 px storey pitch). The tank_house view gives about 3 storeys by door scale. This is close to the source's about 2.5–3.
   - Chimneys: now clear the balustrade by about 0.5–1 storey (left_side about 1, front about 0.5 through perspective). That is still a little short and slim against the source (finding 7).
2. **Pyramid with radial seams and no hips, salmon colour**: **resolved for geometry; colour partly resolved (P2).** The top view shows four flat faces meeting at hips. Seams run straight up each face, perpendicular to each eave (vertical in plan on N/S, horizontal on E/W). The colour is now brown, not salmon, but tan/khaki rather than the source's mauve bronze-brown (finding 6).
3. **Quoins stopping short and black gaps where the top-storey band, cornice and corner pedestal meet**: **partly resolved; still P1.** Quoins now run to the corner and return (architecture_close, fire_balconies), and the belt and top-storey bands return round the corner. A black slit remains at the cornice/corner-pedestal joint, and the coronas are stepped and mismatched (cornice_balustrade, architecture_close; finding 3).
4. **Balcony railings hovering, no bottom rail**: **resolved.** Pickets stand on the deck between a bottom rail and a top rail on every balcony seen (fire_balconies, facade_close, cornice_balustrade).
5. **South balcony stack three bays west of the entrance; narrow flush stone bay; heavy stone band missing**: **partly resolved; new P1s introduced.**
   - The stack is now directly over the marquee (resolved).
   - A projecting limestone-framed bay sits beside it (resolved in kind, one window wide).
   - The "heavy stone pier" was added as a full-height mid-face pier. It cuts a window column to half width (finding 1), and the whole group is mid-face instead of at the SE corner where the front and oblique sources put it. The east-face corner stone bay is also missing (finding 2).
