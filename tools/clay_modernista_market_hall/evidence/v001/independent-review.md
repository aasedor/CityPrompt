# Independent holistic review: clay-modernista-market-hall, v001

Reviewer: independent RLASM v6.1 holistic reviewer (not the builder). I judged only rendered pixels against the three locked source images (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`). I opened all 18 renders and all 4 phone boards, and cropped the zones that were in doubt (the front left edge, the base of the lantern in `front_corner` and the lantern in `top`).

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The overall Greek-cross plan, the single storey, the plaza and the awnings read correctly. But every great gable has an inverted silhouette, with a round iron hoop standing above and outside a pointed gable. The lantern sits over open, unroofed valleys that show the interior to the sky. Several identity materials (stained-glass tympanum, crest and coat of arms, roof cladding and clerestories, tiled dado) are also off-source.

Counts: P0 = 2, P1 = 8, P2 = 6

## 4. Camera validity

- **front:** the asset is clipped at the left frame edge (west-face awning and tree) and there is almost no margin on the right (#10, P1). Everything else is legible.
- **glass_close:** legible, but dominated by reflection shards and an interior seen through glass. It is a weak test of glazing depth (P2, #11).
- **rear_court:** a foreground tree partly occludes the frame (P2, #12). The rear gable is legible.
- **left_side, right_side, rear:** valid, though the building fills only about 45% of the frame width.
- **front_corner, aerial, rear_side, top, facade_close, architecture_close, great_arch, aisle_arcade, lantern_roofs, crest_contact, plaza_awnings, interior:** valid, with the target legible and not occluded or clipped.
