# Independent holistic review: Modernista market hall, v004 (RLASM v6.1 clay)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, plus the three locked sources and the four phone boards. I also made zoomed crops of the front arch, the lantern/roof junction, the aerial lantern, the interior ceiling, the gable pier tops and the aisle dado. Only pixels were judged. Builder claims, metadata and counts were not accepted as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: all three v003 P1s are fixed in the pixels, but the great-arch gable face, the aisle dado and the gable pier tops still have clearly visible fidelity and contact defects against the locked front.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 10

## 5. Previous-version findings (v003): resolved in the pixels?

1. **Interior ceiling was an opaque brown soffit with no lantern glazing or trusses (second attempt): RESOLVED. The defect does not survive the second fix.** In interior.png the glazed lantern clerestory is plainly visible overhead with mullions and transoms. It is bright but not clipped. A gabled iron truss frames the lantern edge, and curved iron nave trusses span the nave below it. The wing-roof soffits are still opaque, but that matches the opaque metal wing roofs now shown on the exterior. Only the blind roof monitors remain, as P2-6.
2. **Lantern corner posts stopped mid-air over a blown-out white patch, with blank triangles and no sill: RESOLVED.** In lantern_roofs and aerial, zoomed, the lantern glazing ends on a sill that follows the wing-roof profiles down into each valley. The corner posts run down to the valley. The mullions and transoms continue through the triangular panes, and the interior trusses can be seen through them. There is no white patch. The only remaining issue is the posts' flat black shading (P2-8).
3. **Quadrant pyramid glass was a few huge saturated primary triangles: RESOLVED.** In architecture_close, top, front_corner and lantern_roofs, each facet has an opaque metal outer zone and a grid of small panes in muted teal, aqua and amber on dark leading. The remaining difference is triangular rather than rectangular panes (P2-5).
4. **v003 P2-4 and P2-5 (wing roofs to opaque standing-seam metal, metal lantern pyramid): VERIFIED.** top, aerial and lantern_roofs show ribbed metal wing roofs and a metal lantern pyramid, consistent with the locked top. The locked top's glazed patches on the wing roofs are carried by the raised monitors.

Note: the fix to v003 P1-3 was not carried over to the tympana. The great-arch glass still uses the same "few huge saturated primaries" grammar that v003 rejected on the quadrants. This is now P1-1.
