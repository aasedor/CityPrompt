# Independent holistic review: clay-ctrain-elevated-station v001 (RLASM v6.1)

I am the independent reviewer and did not build this candidate. I judged rendered pixels only, against the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`). I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, plus the four phone boards. I made zoomed crops only in a new folder: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/ctrain-v001-review-crops/`. I did not use metadata, hashes or builder notes as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the main identity reads correctly: brick headhouse, twin elevated side platforms, glazed canopies, truss skywalks, red pylons and a two-car train. However, the interior camera shows nothing legible (P0). There are also six P1 defects:
- the stair-tower glazing is on the wrong face and too narrow;
- both skywalk junctions are misplaced;
- the guideway reads as a blocked stub;
- the street stairs float, with no structure;
- the whole-envelope cameras do not match the source roles.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 6, P2 = 8
