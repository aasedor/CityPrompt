## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the east wing, west face, balustrades and front camera are now fixed, but the twisting tower still does not read as the source tower. From the front, front_corner, aerial and top it is a top-heavy goblet under a closed helmet or dome cap, not a spiralling-blade spindle ending in an open loop. Eight P1 coherence and fidelity defects remain, among them the south-wing dip, contact voids and pier stubs.
