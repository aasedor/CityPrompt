# Independent holistic review: sblk v001 (clay sculptural superblock)

Reviewer: independent RLASM v6.1 holistic reviewer (not the builder). Evidence: the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), all 18 renders at full resolution, and the four phone boards. Zoomed crops (read-only derivatives) are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-sblk-v001-crops/`. Metadata and builder notes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the perimeter block, the sinuous corner bands and the swooping south roof line are present. But the signature south-east tower is a straight stack of coaxial glass discs with one straight strap, not a twisting, multi-bladed white tower. The east wing is also cut away beside the tower, which exposes sliced floors on a face the top source shows as continuous.

Counts: P0 = 2, P1 = 12, P2 = 5
