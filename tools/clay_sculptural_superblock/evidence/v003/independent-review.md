# Independent holistic review: clay-sculptural-superblock v003 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). I judged only rendered pixels against the locked sources `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I opened all 18 renders and the 4 phone boards at full resolution. My zoomed crops are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/sblk-v003-indep-crops-r7/`.

## 1. Verdict

**Verdict: VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The perimeter block, punched-window street faces, corner balcony bands and tower position are now credible. But the tower still ends in a capped dish inside a horizontal ring, not an open loop. The south-wing cleft is still a sharp V filled by a blank, faceted funnel with exposed slab cuts. The retail piers read as a sawtooth of detached wedges.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 6, P2 = 9
