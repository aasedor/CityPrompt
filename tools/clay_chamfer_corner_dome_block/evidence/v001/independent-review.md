# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — chamfer / v001 (clay)

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Evidence: rendered pixels in `renders/*.png` and `boards/*.png` compared against `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg` only. Builder notes, counts and hashes ignored.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the massing, chamfer, dome and flat terracotta roof are recognisably the source block, but the wings carry one to two more storeys than any source shows, the drum glazing is stuck-on boxes that poke through the drum wall, and first-floor railing posts hang through the balcony slab, so the candidate fails on envelope, opening and contact grounds before fidelity is considered.
