# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — clay-chamfer-corner-dome-block v004

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence used: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png`, the four `boards/*.png`. Every render and source was opened at full resolution; working crops are under `scratchpad/review-crops-chamfer-v004/`. Builder notes, counts and hashes were ignored as proof. Pixel coordinates below are in the native image (sources: 1024x768 front, 1195x896 oblique/top; renders 1440x1080).

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the locked sources show a three-storey block (rusticated ground floor + a brick storey with a continuous balcony + a brick storey with individual balconies) and the candidate renders four storeys, so the envelope carries one extra storey; three further P1 fidelity defects (windowed drum, bay glazing without its iron railings, ground-floor openings wide instead of tall) remain on the identity elements.
