# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: chamfer corner dome block, v007

Reviewer: independent (did not build; no build code, reports, README or VERSIONS read). Evidence: the three locked sources, all 18 renders at full resolution, and the four phone boards. Pixel coordinates refer to the 1440x1080 renders unless stated.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the envelope, storey count, cornice and parapet, balconies and chamfer entrance now match the sources. The dome crown, though, still misreads the source in four clearly visible ways: a dark panel band instead of pierced copper cresting, cream castellated dormers, ribs that stand off the shell, and a three-facet bay instead of a bowed five-light bay. The front camera also does not match the source's chamfer-on role.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 9**

Storey count, verified in the source pixels: front.png (left wing x 200-400, right wing x 660-850) and oblique.jpg (both wings) each show **three** storeys. These are a stone ground floor, a brick first floor with a continuous iron balcony, and a brick second floor with individual balconies, then the cornice and balustrade. On the chamfer the bay has two glazed levels over the arched entrance. The brief's prose ("five-storey ... three storeys of warm brick", "stone piano nobile") does not match the locked pixels. The sources also show a **brick** first floor, and the ground-floor shop openings are **square-headed**; only the chamfer entrance is arched. I judged against the pixels. The render's three storeys (front.png rows at y 780-910, 670-760 and 540-630) conform.

## 4. Camera validity

- No view has the target occluded, clipped or illegible. All 18 renders show the intended subject, and the interior view reads correctly (a French window with a railing outside).
- front.png is an invalid **role match**: a side elevation with the chamfer in profile, where the source is a chamfer-centred eye-level view (finding 5).
- aerial.png and front_corner.png are legible but small in frame. aerial is aimed from the rear quadrant (P2-g).
