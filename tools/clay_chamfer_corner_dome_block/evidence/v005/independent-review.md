# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — chamfer corner dome block, v005

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence used: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 files in `renders/`; the four boards in `boards/`. Nothing else was read. Every statement below was checked in pixels; pixel coordinates are in the full-resolution render (1440×1080) or source (front 1024×768, oblique/top 1195×896).

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the lantern floats above the dome with a continuous band of background between them in every view that shows the apex, and the chamfer composition (dome profile + invented stone drum, flat-headed entrance, flat cornice) still misreads the locked sources.

Counts: **P0 = 1, P1 = 3, P2 = 8.**

### Storey reading (my own count, before anything else)

- `sources/front.png`, left wing (x≈190–430): shop floor y≈560–700; a row of tall French windows with one **continuous** iron balcony y≈445–545 (rail ≈520–545); a row with **individual** iron balconies y≈355–435; bracketed cornice y≈300–345; balustrade y≈255–290. Right wing (x≈640–850): shop floor; row y≈440–540 (balconies ≈520–550); row y≈335–430 (balconies ≈400–440); cornice; balustrade. No further window row exists between the upper row and the cornice.
- `sources/oblique.jpg`, both street faces (x≈240–560 and x≈700–1000, y≈380–896): shop floor, one upper row behind a continuous balcony, one upper row with individual balconies, cornice, balustrade.
- Bow bay (front x≈430–600; oblique x≈450–760): arched entrance at ground + two glazed levels, each with an iron balcony, then cornice and dome.

**Reading: ground + two upper storeys under the cornice (three storeys).** The narrative in the brief ("five-storey", "three storeys of warm brick") does not match the locked pixels; the sources govern. The render has ground + two upper storeys (`front.png` ground y≈730–860, rows y≈620–720 and y≈500–590), so the v004 P0 is resolved.
