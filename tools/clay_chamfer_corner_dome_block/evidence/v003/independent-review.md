# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — chamfer / v003 (clay-chamfer-corner-dome-block)

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 `renders/*.png` opened at 1440x1080; `boards/phone-0[1-4]*.png`. Working crops under `scratchpad/review-crops-chamfer-v003/`. Pixel coordinates below are (x, y) in the 1440x1080 render or in the source at native size. Builder notes, counts and hashes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the candidate carries one storey more than every locked source shows, still has pure-black voids where the parapet balustrade meets the bay's cornice ring, and ships an `interior` view in which nothing is legible; the v002 P1s on the drum/dome proportion and the pier-dominant bow are not resolved.
