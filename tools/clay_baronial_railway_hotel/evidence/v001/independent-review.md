# Independent holistic review: clay-baronial-railway-hotel v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence used: only the pixels of `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution, and the 4 phone boards. I did not use builder metadata, counts, hashes or notes as proof. Pixel coordinates refer to the 1440x1080 renders unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the envelope, openings and contact fail. There is an extra storey, the porte-cochere is blind, there are see-through voids where wings meet, the tourelle windows are applied black slabs, the crow-step copes float, and the interior view is black. The Baronial identity is also under-scaled: the crow-stepped gables are dormer-sized, the intermediate tourelles are missing and the window grid is too dense.

Counts: P0 = 7, P1 = 10, P2 = 9

## 4. Camera validity

- **interior**: invalid. The frame is almost black (mean luminance 9/255) and nothing is legible (P0, finding 7).
- **rear_court**: weak. It is underexposed and a central tree hides about 35% of the frame and the target corner (P1, finding 17).
- **front**: clipped. Spire and cap apices touch the top edge, the bottom quarter is empty ground, and the viewpoint is mirrored relative to the locked front (P1, finding 15).
- **front_corner**: viewpoint mirrored relative to the locked oblique (P1, finding 15). The building is otherwise fully in frame.
- **entrance_tower**: the caps are clipped at the top edge (P2).
- aerial, top, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side, facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, porte_cochere, corner_tourelle, crow_gables, roof_court: valid. The target is legible and unoccluded.
