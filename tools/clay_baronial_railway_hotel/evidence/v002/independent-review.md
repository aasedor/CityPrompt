# Independent holistic review: clay Scottish Baronial railway hotel, v002

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Basis: the rendered pixels of all 18 `renders/*.png`, the 4 `boards/*.png` and the locked `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, each opened at full resolution, with zoomed crops of the tower junction, the court corners, the crow-stepped gables and the tower head. I sampled pixel values to confirm the voids are true black. The builder's notes, counts and claims were not used as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: v002 fixes the black interior, the blind porte-cochère, the slab tourelle windows, the lawn court and the stray parapet cube. Three P0s remain: the pure-black void at the tower junction is unchanged, the crow-stepped gables have copes that cut through their windows and black holes in them, and the walls carry one more full storey than the locked sources.

Counts: P0 = 3, P1 = 8, P2 = 6

## 4. Camera validity

- **No view is invalid**: every target is legible and none is occluded by trees. The v001 problems are fixed: interior is lit, rear_court is exposed and unblocked.
- **front:** valid, weakly framed. The lower 45% is empty ground and the west end is clipped at the frame edge (P2).
- **front_corner:** valid, but mirrored relative to the oblique source. aerial is the true matched-handedness view (P2).
- **glass_close:** valid but marginal. Only one window is fully legible; most of the frame is wall and a tree.
- **rear_court:** valid and legible. It shows the court face of the passage, not the court as a space, which roof_court and aerial cover.
- **interior:** valid, lit and legible.
- All other views (aerial, top, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side, facade_close, architecture_close, entrance_tower, porte_cochere, corner_tourelle, crow_gables, roof_court) are valid and legible.
