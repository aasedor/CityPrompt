# Independent holistic review: clay Baronial railway hotel, v005

Reviewer: independent RLASM v6.1 holistic reviewer (not the builder).
Inputs I opened: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution, all 4 `boards/*.png`. I also made enlarged crops of the porte-cochere arch, the passage, the porch threshold, the tower and porch decks, and the court-side arch, and measured near-black pixels (RGB sum < 45) in every render. I did not use metadata, counts, hashes or the builder's notes as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: both v004 P1s are fixed in the pixels, but the porte-cochere still fails in two places. At the porch threshold the masonry does not meet the ground: there is a pure-black slot and the lawn shows under it. Inside the passage, pure-black void seams run along the vault-to-wall and court-arch-to-vault junctions.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 2, P2 = 14
