# Independent holistic review: clay-zinc-dome-corner-block v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent; I did not build this. I compared rendered pixels with the locked source pixels only (sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg, sources/top.jpg). I opened all 18 renders at full resolution and checked the 4 phone boards. I sampled pixels and made zoomed crops of the dome base, the chamfer, the awning plan and the dormers.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the brick-and-stone Haussmann envelope, storey program and balcony rhythm match the sources. However, the corner identity does not. The red café awning is cut into three detached segments instead of wrapping the chamfer. The dome sits inboard of the chamfer on a drum buried in the mansard. The oculus dormer is reduced to a flat disc. Pure-black faces mark every dormer and the awning valances. The rear_court camera never shows the court.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 13

## 4. Camera validity

- **rear_court: invalid for its role.** It shows the exterior rear street facade instead of the inner court, so the court target is never shown (#5).
- interior: legible but heavily underexposed (P2, #16).
- right_side: valid, but it shows the east non-source face. The south street face has no square-on view (P2, #15).
- front, front_corner and aerial are valid but loosely framed, with the asset at about 45–50% of the frame width (P2, #14).
- architecture_close crops the top of the dome at the frame edge. This is acceptable because its target is the chamfer facade, and corner_dome covers the dome in full.
- All other views (top, left_side, rear, rear_side, facade_close, glass_close, corner_dome, cafe_awning, balcony_ironwork, mansard_dormers, roof_court) are unoccluded, unclipped and legible.
