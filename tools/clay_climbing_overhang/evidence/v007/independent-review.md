# Independent holistic review: The Overhang, v007 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent. I did not build this candidate. I opened all 18 renders and the three locked sources at full resolution, plus the four phone boards. I judged rendered pixels only. I did not treat the builder's IoU figures, overlays, dimensions or notes as proof, and I did not open the overlays.

## 1. Verdict

**PASS_ZERO_P0_P1**: every identity element is present in matched views and reads as the locked building. The shell's foot, lean, folded faces, overhang, level open rim and contact match the front and oblique sources closely. The remaining deviations are polish items: the colour of the timber, some secondary landscape items and the junctions on faces that are seen least.

- **rear:** it is close and low, but it shows the whole north elevation.
- **Interior and close-up views** (interior, glass_close, facade_close): each shows its intended subject legibly.
- **Phone board 01:** it correctly pairs locked front, oblique and top with the front, front_corner and top renders.
