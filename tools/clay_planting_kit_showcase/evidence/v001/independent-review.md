# Independent RLASM v6.1 review: shared planting kit showcase v001

Candidate: `clay-planting-kit-showcase-clay-v001` (showcase of the shared planting kit, not a catalogue building)
Reviewer: independent. I did not build this model. I opened all 10 renders at full 1440 x 1080 resolution and both phone boards. I also read the four park families' independent reviews and opened their planting renders: Prince's Island v003 `promenade_beds`, Halifax v003 `carpet_beds`, green bridge v003 `planting_bed` and stepped plaza v003 `architecture_close`. For the reference I used botanical-garden `variant_0.png`. `variant_0_angle_60.jpg` is a Git LFS pointer file, not an image, so it could not be viewed. I wrote zoom crops only to the new folder `review/crops_qzvmtk/` inside the v001 folder. The build report, GLB JSON chunk and kit source (`scripts/family_plantkit.py`) were used only to explain causes and suggest fixes. The verdict rests on the pixels.

Verdict: FAIL

Counts: P0=0 P1=6 P2=9

Summary: The kit makes real progress. The daisy heads (coneflower and rudbeckia), the salvia spikes, the ovate leaf mounds and the fine arching grass blades are a clear step beyond the four families' cones, straw ribbons and gems. The Prince's-Island-style perennial bed with its groundcover underlay is the strongest view. But five kit elements still show the faceted or puffy primitive reading that stopped the families: the shrub mass, the mophead hydrangea, the yarrow umbel, the feather-reed plume and the bedding/carpet field. The kit's own placement rule also lets a shrub swallow a kerb. Near-black pixel clusters were all checked. Every one is a contact or under-leaf occlusion shadow, not a black patch, slot or wedge.

- **front_corner** (−15.5, −17.0, 9.5 → −1.0, −1.0, 0.2, 24 mm). An elevated oblique from outside the south-west corner. All eight beds, both walks and the lawn edge are in frame, nothing is clipped, and there is no ground plane past the lawn in the lower frame. Valid.
- **top** (orthographic, scale 31 m, at 60 m). Covers the full 29 × 22 m lawn with an even margin. Valid.
- **eye_flower_heads, eye_perennial_drift, eye_arching_grass, eye_shrub_mass** (z = 1.6 m, at y −9.6 to −10.3, 30 mm). Each stands on the lawn 1.4-2.0 m south of its bed's kerb, inside the site (SY0 = −10.5), and is aimed at the bed centre. Each bed is wholly or almost wholly in frame. Valid pedestrian views.
- **eye_groundcover, eye_bedding_band** (z = 1.6 m, y −1.9, on the southern gravel walk, 30 mm). They look down 1-1.5 m onto their beds. Valid.
- **eye_carpet_bed** (0.6, −1.7, 1.6 → 4.8, 1.3, 0.15, 24 mm). On the walk, raking along the leaf-shaped bed. This is the right view to expose the crest teeth. Valid.
- **eye_perennial_bed** (−2.8, 3.9, 1.6 → 0.5, 8.0, 0.4, 24 mm). On the lawn just south of the north walk. The nearest foliage is cut at the left frame edge, which is acceptable for a pedestrian view. Valid.
- No camera is inside geometry, below grade or outside the site. Every eye camera is at 1.6 m as the brief states.
- Gaps (P2-level, not counted): every eye view looks north, with the key light from the south-west, so there is no backlit view. There is no oblique close-up of the hydrangea or yarrow at 1-2 m, and no eye view of B8's rear kerb, where the clearance defect sits. The next round should add `eye_b8_rear` (looking south at the backdrop shrubs and kerb) and one close specimen view.

## 6. Per-family closure

**Prince's Island Park (v003 P1: faceted cone flower heads, flat straw-ribbon grasses, bare fill between mounds).** *Partially closable; not closed by v001.*
- Flower heads: the daisy and spike heads would close the cone-head reading.
- Grasses: the arching blade tufts would close the straw-ribbon reading.
- Bare fill: the groundcover underlay would close it, as B8 shows, if it becomes the drift default (P2 #9).
- Still reproduces the failure: the plume pods (P1 #5), which are the "feathery plume grasses" in the source; the crumpled shrub mounds (P1 #1); and the yarrow plates (P1 #4).
- Missing kit element: a **white panicle head** (loose conical panicle, as in astilbe or panicle hydrangea), which the Prince's Island review names in the source.

**Halifax Public Gardens (v002/v003 P1: carpet-bed plants read as faceted gems with hard outlines).** *Not closable with v001.*
- The bedding bands swap gems for sausages, with saw-tooth crests and mulch valleys (P1 #2).
- The mophead hydrangea, which forms the white spine of the beds, is still a crumpled faceted head (P1 #3).
- Missing kit element: a **flat clipped carpet-bed module** with dense micro-florets (begonia, ageratum, alyssum) at 150-300 per m², with a shared-edge band rule and no soil valleys.
- Also missing: a **low mounded bedding-plant cushion** (0.25-0.4 m smooth mound with floret skin) for the ring and strip beds.

**Planted green bridge (v002 boulders; v003 gumdrop mounds, solid lilac capsules, kerb and rail overrun).** *Not closable with v001.*
- The shrub mass still reads as lumps (P1 #1), so the gumdrop and boulder risk carries straight over.
- The placement rule lets shrubs cut through kerbs (P1 #6), which repeats v003 P1 #3/#5.
- The fountain grass would supply the missing bunch-grass layer, once a bright yellow-green variant is added.
- Missing kit elements:
  - a **lavender prototype**: a grey-sage, narrow-leaf low mound with a dense haze of thin purple spikes, drift-capable. The salvia's broad ovate rosette is the wrong foliage.
  - a **poppy or cup head** type for the orange accents.
  - a **shrub size range from 0.4 m fillers to 1.6 m**.
  - a clearance rule that also covers railings.

**Stepped terraced plaza (v003 P1: bougainvillea as faceted balloons, no drape).** *Not closable with v001.* The kit has no climbing or draping element at all. If the shrub_mass approach were reused it would reproduce the balloon clusters. Missing kit elements:
- a **draping climber / wall-trained canopy module**: a branching woody armature that follows the wall plane, the roof parapet and the corner, and never crosses door or window openings;
- a continuous canopy shell carrying dense small bract clusters (≤ 0.15 m) with a hanging curtain fringe;
- a parapet-cascade mode that runs the drape several terraces down a coping;
- a **potted geranium / pot-plant module** for the parapet pots.
