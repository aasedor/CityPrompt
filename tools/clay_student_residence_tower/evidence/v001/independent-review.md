# Independent holistic review: clay-student-residence-tower v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent; I did not build this candidate. I judged only the rendered pixels against the locked source pixels (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`). I opened all 18 renders and phone board 01 at full resolution. I made zoomed crops only in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/indep-review-crops-stud-v001-r1/`. I did not read the metadata, the counts or the builder notes, and I did not use them as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the white wall mass, which is the tower's main identity element, is in the wrong place and has the wrong proportions and shape. It is a square corner pier that takes about a third of both the south and the west faces. In the sources it is a tapered, leaning blade that fills most of the west face, and the south face is all glass from the corner. There are also several P1 contact, finish and landscape defects.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 8, P2 = 8

## 4. Camera validity

- No view has its target fully occluded, and no view clips the asset. Every view is legible.
- `front` is valid but **role-mismatched**. It is a frontal south elevation, not the source's street-level SW corner view, so the west face cannot be compared in the matched role (P2, finding 17).
- `front_corner` and `aerial` are valid but distant and high. The tower fills only ~35–50% of the frame height, and `aerial` nearly duplicates `top` (P2).
- The detail views (`facade_close`, `architecture_close`, `glass_close`, `glazed_base`, `slab_edges`, `planted_terrace`, `roof_dome`, `lattice_screen`, `interior`, `rear_north`) are all valid and legible. In `glazed_base` a foreground tree partly covers the centre bay but does not hide the base grammar. The `interior` view legibly shows a study desk and chair behind full-height glazing.
