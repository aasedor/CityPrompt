# Independent holistic review: clay-student-residence-tower v003 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent; I did not build this candidate. I judged only the rendered pixels against the locked source pixels (sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg, sources/top.jpg). I opened all 18 renders and the 4 phone boards at full resolution. I made zoomed crops only into a new folder, `scratchpad/indep-review-v003-crops/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The envelope, storey count, blade, glazed base and plan proportion now conform, and three of the four v002 findings are resolved. The roof crown still reads as a different object from the source: a small circular globe-ribbed dome in a lawn ring, an undersized low penthouse and a rail-height screen. The terraces are also wrong: they are hung boxes stacked on most west-face floors instead of a few recessed loggias.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 4, P2 = 9

## 4. Camera validity

- **No view is invalid** (no P0). No target is fully occluded, no asset is clipped, and everything is legible.
- **front:** A valid eye-level wide view from the south-west street corner. The blade is left of centre and dominant, and the south face is to the right. It matches the source front composition.
- **glazed_base:** A street tree centred in the frame occludes about 25% of the base. The target is still legible (P2 #10).
- **front_corner and aerial:** Valid, but the tower is small in frame (P2 #11).
- **planted_terrace:** It frames a terrace (fixing the v002 framing finding), but so tightly that its location on the building cannot be read. It also exposes the hung-box defect (P1 #4).
- **interior:** Valid but has no exterior read (P2 #12).
- **All other views are valid:** top, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side, facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, slab_edges, roof_dome, lattice_screen and rear_north.
