# Independent holistic review: railtruss v001 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent; did not build this candidate. Judged only rendered pixels (18 renders, 4 phone boards) against the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`). Builder metadata, counts and notes were not used as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The plan, street section, through-arch end span, twin tracks and train massing read correctly. Five visible defects still block production approval:
- the arch and spandrel grammar is wrong;
- the transverse bents are X-braced and the sources show no such bracing;
- the train windows are flat paint;
- there are black voids at the through-arch bearings;
- the arch crown is not seated on the deck.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 9

## 2. Findings table

| # | severity | view(s) | symptom (what the pixels show) | likely cause | finite fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P1 | facade_close, arch_span, lattice_detail, architecture_close, right_side, left_side, front | Each deck span is drawn as a double-chord **trussed arch ring**. The lattice sits inside the ring, between two concentric curved chords, and the ring's top chord runs almost tangent to the deck girder. Above the ring the spandrel holds only short radial posts and a fan of struts at the haunches. The sources (front most clearly, also oblique) show a different system: a single heavy **solid riveted plate rib**, with the large triangular spandrel above it filled by an open lattice of vertical posts and long N/diagonal members up to the deck girder. As rendered, the identity element "latticed spandrels" reads as a braced ring rather than an open spandrel web. | The arch assembly models the rib as a Warren-trussed ring, and the spandrel fill is reduced to bracket struts. | Rebuild the deck-span assembly in three parts. (a) A solid built-up rib (plate web with flange edges), one deep member about 0.6–0.8 m. (b) Spandrel lattice between the rib extrados and the deck girder: vertical posts at about 1/8-span pitch plus N-diagonals. Its depth should run from 0 at the crown to roughly a quarter of the column height at the springing. (c) No lattice inside the rib. Re-check against source front at the near span. |
| 2 | P1 | front, street_under, column_base, rear_north, rear, rear_side, front_corner (end bents) | Every transverse bent has **full-height X-bracing** between the two column lines, from the cap beam down to just above the pedestals, plus a mid-height strut. These Xs dominate the street-level views (street_under, front, column_base) and make the structure read as a generic trestle-bent El. They also cut across the paved footway/plaza under the deck that the sources show open. In source front, people walk between pedestals under the viaduct, and the transverse bays show arched portals with no diagonal bracing to ground. | A generic bent-bracing module was applied to every bent. | Delete the lower X-bracing below the springing level on all intermediate bents. Replace it with a transverse latticed portal arch (rib plus spandrel lattice, matching the longitudinal spans) between the paired columns, or with a knee-braced cap girder above the springing. Keep bracing, if needed, above about 5 m clear. Verify clear passage under the deck in street_under and rear_north. |
| 3 | P1 | train_cars, glass_close, left_side, right_side, front_corner | The train has **no window openings**. The window band is a flat dark-grey painted stripe with no reveal, sill or return. The doors are flat grey plates standing proud of the body, not recessed leaves with glazing. The source shows silver cars with distinct dark glazed windows, recessed doors with door windows, and cab-end glazing. glass_close, the one glazing close-up, shows only a flat stripe. | Train car built as a box with decal-like stripe/plate geometry. | Model per-car window openings as insets about 3–5 cm deep with frame returns, separated by body-colour piers. Make doors recessed leaves with their own glazing, and add an inset cab windscreen and headlight recesses at each end car. Keep the clay palette. |

| 5 | P1 | lattice_detail (crown), facade_close, arch_span | **Arch crown not seated.** A continuous dark slit runs along the crown between the arch top chord and the deck stringer/fascia girder. Trim the segments that penetrate the girder, and re-render lattice_detail at the same camera. |
| 6 | P2 | column_base, facade_close, architecture_close | No rivet rows, batten/lacing plates or flange plate edges. ## 4. Camera validity

- **No view is invalid.** The target is legible in all 18 renders, and no view shows the target fully occluded.
- **front_corner:** the near end bent's feet are clipped by the bottom frame edge (P2 #12).
- **top:** not a plan view. Deck parallax hides the red lane and the street-side column line (P2 #13).
- **left_side:** a foreground roof covers the lower part of the frame and partly occludes the west columns (P2 #13). The arches and deck are still legible.
- **front:** valid, but composed from the roadway looking up at the soffit, so it does not match the source front's footway elevation (P2 #13).
- **glass_close:** legible. It frames the train side through the through-arch members, and it is the view that proves the windows have no depth (P1 #3).
- **rear:** the target is small but legible.
