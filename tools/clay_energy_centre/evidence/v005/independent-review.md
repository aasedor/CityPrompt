# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: clay energy centre v005

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** v005 fixes all six v004 P1s in the pixels, but five new P1 defects are visible: the hourglass stack, the west corner pier and bay rhythm, the north annex, the duct runs and the south-face storey split.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 8**

Evidence used: sources `front.png`, `oblique.jpg` and `top.jpg`, all 18 renders and all 4 phone boards, each read at full resolution with crops of the critical areas. Pixel coordinates are in each file's native resolution (renders 1440x1080, front source 1024x768, oblique and top sources 1195x896).
