# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: clay energy centre v004

**Verdict: VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** Version 4 fixes the riser pipe and the north service bay. It also removes the entrance fin and canopy. Six P1 defects remain, all visible in the pixels: the south-face bay rhythm, the placement of the entrance bay and its head, the threshold slab, how the concrete panel joints read, the corten colour, and the position of the slot window.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 6, P2 = 9**

Evidence used: sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg and sources/top.jpg, plus all 18 renders at 1440x1080 and the 4 phone boards. Some regions were cropped and enlarged for close inspection. I did not read any build code, reports or builder notes.

Orientation, read from the sources: top.jpg shows the streets on the south (bottom) and west (left) sides, with the corten block forming the east half of the plan. front.png looks from the southwest: the left part of the image is the long west face, the corner falls at about x=630, and the short south face plus the corten block sit at x=630–940. The renders follow the same orientation: left_side is the west face, front is the south face, right_side is the east face and rear is the north face.

- No view has its target hidden, clipped or illegible. Every render frames its subject with the asset fully inside the frame.
- corten_block.png crops the base of the slot at the bottom edge (y 1080), which is acceptable for a detail view. The tree partly overlaps the lower left of the block but does not hide the slot.
- interior.png is legible but shows only a narrow slice of the plant room.
- Role mismatch (P2, F15): the render "front" is the south elevation, not the source's southwest three-quarter view.
