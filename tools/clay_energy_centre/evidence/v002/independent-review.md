# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — energy centre clay candidate v002

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Judged only rendered pixels in `renders/*.png` and `boards/*.png` against the locked pixels in `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`. Builder notes, counts and hashes were not used as evidence. All 18 renders, the 3 sources and the 4 boards were opened at full resolution.

Orientation used below: the street corner is the SW corner (top source: streets along the left/west and bottom/south edges). "West face" = the long glazed face on the left of the front source; "south face" = the face that runs from the corner to the corten block. The corten block is the east volume.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the candidate has the right massing, roof plant and corten block, but the lower volume's south envelope is open for roughly a third of its length (exposed floor plates, interior visible from the street), the board-formed concrete corner panel is absent (a dark slatted louvre sits in the wrong bay instead), and the building has no public entrance at all.
