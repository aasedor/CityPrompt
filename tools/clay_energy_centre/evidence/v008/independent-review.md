# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review — energy centre clay candidate v008

Reviewer: independent (no relationship with the builder). Evidence used: `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`; all 18 `renders/*.png`; `boards/phone-01..04`. No build code, reports, README or VERSIONS files were read. Every measurement below was taken from the pixels (grid-overlaid crops and scanline sampling), not from the builder's notes.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — topology, storey count, identity elements, contacts and cameras are all sound and the v007 bay-rhythm P1 is fixed, but the lower brick/glass volume is rendered markedly too squat: its south elevation is about 1.6× as wide as it is tall, whereas both perspective sources show that face at roughly 0.65–1.05 wide-to-tall, which turns the signature corner concrete panel into a 4.5:1 strip and is plainly visible in the matched front and corner views.

Counts: **P0 = 0, P1 = 1, P2 = 11.**
