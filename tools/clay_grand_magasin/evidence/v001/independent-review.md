# Independent holistic review: clay-grand-magasin v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. Evidence used: only the pixels of `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution (plus enlarged crops of corner_dome, architecture_close and front) and the four phone boards. Builder metadata, counts, hashes and notes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the corner drum, the identity element that crowns the curved glazed bay, is sunk into the mansard slope with its posts stabbing through the roof. On top of that, eight P1 fidelity, material and coherence defects remain: pale flat zinc roof, undersized off-centre light well, missing gilded cornice balustrade, missing flanking pavilions, weak gilded balconies, black dormer cheeks and a mis-targeted court camera.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 8, P2 = 9

## 4. Camera validity

- **rear_court: invalid target.** It frames the outer north or east street facade, not the inner court or light well (F9).
- **interior: marginal.** It is legible but heavily underexposed and shows only the rear punched-window wall, a column and a box (F17, P2).
- **top: valid but uninformative for the court.** The light well renders as a solid black void, so its floor and walls are illegible (F4, F9).
- **front: valid.** The asset is unclipped and centred but small in frame (about 55% width). It is legible enough to compare roles with front.png.
- **front_corner, aerial, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side: valid.** The whole envelope is in frame, nothing is clipped and nothing is occluded.
- **facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, corner_dome, marquise_sign, gilded_balconies, mansard_dormers, roof_domes: valid.** Each target is legible and unoccluded. corner_dome and roof_domes are what expose the P0 drum intersection.
- **Boards:** phone-01 pairs locked top with render aerial, so the top-vs-top role match is missing from the board (F18, P2). The render top itself was reviewed directly.
