# Independent holistic review: clay-grand-magasin v006 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. Inputs I opened at full resolution: the three locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), all 18 renders in `renders/`, and the four phone boards in `boards/`. I also made zoomed crops into the new folder `review/indep-crops/`. I took nothing on the builder's word; every claim below comes from pixels.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** Both v005 P1s are fixed in the pixels: the drum is no longer buried and the court dome ribs are now continuous. But two P1s still block approval. First, the stone attic storey that the locked front and oblique show between the gilded cornice balustrade and the zinc mansard is missing. The drum's flanks are now sloped zinc cheeks instead of the stone attic walls the source shows. Second, the gilded balcony fronts were simplified to flat boards, and every boss on them is now a pure-black void.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 2, P2 = 10

## 5. Previous-version findings (v005 P1s): resolved in the pixels?

1. **Corner drum sunk in a zinc funnel. RESOLVED as defined; the burial does not survive this second fix.** In corner_dome, roof_domes, front_corner and left_side, the drum's lower tier is now a straight glazed cylinder, not a bowl. Every post runs at full height to the same level, with no mid-tier cuts and no staggered heights, and the tier stands fully exposed down to the balustrade, as in the locked front. The stone plinth is not visible: the balustrade hides it in every view, which is also true of the source, so I am not counting that against it. What remains of the old funnel are two concave zinc cheeks at the drum's sides and a black slot at its rear. These are no longer a burial, but they replace the source's stone attic flanks. They are folded into new P1 #1, whose real cause is the missing attic storey, with the rear slot as P2 #6.
2. **Light-well dome ribs not joining up. RESOLVED.** top and rear_court show 16 continuous ribs from the base ring to the central cap. No short radial rods stop short of the cap. The rear ribs sit on the glass shell; their apparent loops are the dome's shoulder in perspective, and the glass silhouette runs under them. No separate faceted ring crosses them. The angular spacing is slightly uneven and the cap shape is simplified (P2 #5). The missing purlin ring the builder mentioned is not a defect at clay level.

The P0 + P1 total is still 2, the same as v004 and v005, but the two P1s are different defects. The builder's GLB-size simplification of the gilded fronts introduced one of them, a regression.
