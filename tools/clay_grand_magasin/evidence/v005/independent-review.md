# Independent holistic review: clay-grand-magasin v005 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. I judged only the pixels of the 3 locked sources (`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), the 18 renders and the 4 phone boards. I opened every render and source at full resolution. Zoomed crops are in a separate scratch folder (`scratchpad/magasin_v005_review_crops/`). I did not use builder notes, metadata or counts as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the whole-envelope identity now reads correctly and the interior is legible. Two P1 defects remain on the glass domes. In the corner_dome and roof_domes close views, the corner drum still sits sunk in a zinc funnel. In rear_court, the ribs of the light-well dome are broken and floating.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 2, P2 = 11

## 4. Camera validity

- All 18 views are framed on their targets. None is occluded, clipped, or empty.
- interior: now legible. It looks down a lit sales hall past a column line, counters and the street display windows, and the marquise and red band show through the glazing. Some black voids remain (P2 #3), but they do not stop the view from reading.
- architecture_close crops the dome off at the top edge. This is acceptable because the detail is about the bay and facade.
- rear_court is valid: it frames the light-well dome directly. It is the view that exposes P1 #2.
- The trees at the marquise ends stand very close to the canopy edge in front and front_corner. No clear intersection can be proven at this resolution.
