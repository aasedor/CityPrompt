# Independent holistic review: grand magasin v003 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. Evidence: only the pixels of `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` (opened at full resolution, with zoomed crops of the corner crown, dome junctions and wing bays) and the four `boards/*.png`. I did not use builder notes, metadata or counts as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The v002 punched-window and court-dome defects are fixed. The corner crown still differs from the source: it has an extra glazed tier, a gilded band one storey higher than the wings, a low hemispherical dome, and a corner bay with curtain-wall rhythm. The top view also inverts the source's dome hierarchy.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 4, P2 = 10

## 4. Camera validity

No view is invalid. In every one of the 18 renders the target is visible, unclipped and legible.
- The front and front_corner views are matched to source front and oblique in role and direction, though both are a little distant: the building fills about 58% and about 70% of frame width respectively.
- The aerial and top views are matched to the oblique and top sources; the top view is a true nadir with the same corner orientation (corner dome at bottom-left, streets to the south and west).
- The detail views (facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, corner_dome, marquise_sign, gilded_balconies, mansard_dormers, roof_domes, rear_court) each frame their named element without occlusion.
- The interior view is dark but readable: a column, a counter and windows onto the court.
