# Independent holistic review: magasin v004 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent; judged on rendered pixels against `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg` only. I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, the three sources and the four phone boards, and zoomed crops of the crown, corner bay, wings and top view.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the v003 crown, dome-profile, corner-bay and dome-order defects are fixed in the pixels. Two P1s remain. The new glazed drum is buried in the corner mansard: zinc slopes cut through its glazing, its mullions end in spikes, and the "lit room wall" behind it reads as an opaque pale bowl. The `interior` view is illegible.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 2, P2 = 9

## 5. Previous-version (v003) findings: resolved in the pixels?

1. **Three-stage crown, with the corner's gilded band a storey above the wings' balustrade: RESOLVED as stated.** There is no glazed attic stage. The gilded balustrade runs unbroken and level from both wings round the corner (corner_dome, architecture_close, front), and the drum rises behind it. The attic tier does **not** survive this second fix. However, the replacement drum introduces a new P1 (finding 1): the corner mansard swallows the drum's lower half, so the crown still does not show the source's clean "balustrade, then full glazed drum, then dome" sequence in the close views.
2. **Low hemisphere dome (about 0.44): RESOLVED.** Measured about 0.78 in left_side and about 0.85 in corner_dome (height from ring to lantern base over ring width). That is a stilted, ribbed bulb with a lantern, inside the source's 0.7-0.9 band.
3. **Corner bay as a uniform grid of 7-8 narrow lights: RESOLVED.** Two strong dark iron columns with gilded capitals divide the curve into three bays (2, 3 and 2 facets). The uniform-grid defect does **not** survive this second fix. Residual P2 4: the central arch is flat-crowned and the side arches are mostly hidden by the over-thick columns from the front.
4. **Top-view dome sizes reversed: RESOLVED as a reversal; residual scale deviation P2.** Measured in top: corner dome 20% of building width (source 18%), court dome 32% (source 39%), light well 45% (source 52%). The court dome now clearly dominates the corner dome (1.6x; source 2.1x). There are still no source-style bridges round the court dome (P2 5).

Builder's other claims, checked in the pixels:
- Top-storey fronts are dark iron railings: true (architecture_close, mansard_dormers). Note that the source front shows gilded openwork there (P2 9).
- Lit room wall behind the drum glass: present, and part of what makes the drum read as an opaque bowl (P1 1).
- North, east and light-well faces have lighter frames: true (right_side, rear, rear_court). They are coherent with the visible grammar: pilasters, arched top storey, cornice and dormers.
- Two glazing rows per street bay: true (facade_close). Acceptable for clay.
- Fewer relief elements on the gilded fronts: true. The remaining bosses render with black centres (P2 3).
- Wider-spaced brick coursing: not legible in any frame, and no defect seen.
