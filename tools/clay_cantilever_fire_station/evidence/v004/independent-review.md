### Status of the four v003 findings in the v004 pixels

- **Black slot at the SW corner between the corner pier and the red leg** — **not resolved**: a black void remains at the pier/soffit junction in soffit_corner, loggia_close and front_corner (finding 1).
- **Loggia too shallow and short** — **partly resolved**: there is now a C-framed recess with a north return and visible depth, but it is still ≈40 % of the face (source ≈80 %) and framed in proud white slabs with open mitres (findings 2, 3).
- **Red leg on the south face instead of the north end of the west face** — **resolved in position** (north end of the west face, dark base below, lower glazing to its south), but it is an applied proud plate rather than flush skin (finding 6) and short (finding 9).
- **Apparatus bays spanning half the south face instead of three quarters** — **not resolved as a rhythm**: the glazed hall now spans ≈72 %, but as six identical bay modules; the four bollard-marked bays still span ≈48 % (finding 4).

No view is invalid: in every render the named target is in frame, unclipped and legible. Notes that fall short of invalidity:

- `front`, `left_side`, `right_side`, `rear`: the building occupies roughly a quarter of the frame height; bay-level detail needed 4× magnification to count. Under-framed, not illegible.
- `facade_close`: the flagpole crosses the frame at x≈300 in front of the first visible bay; the bays and east leg remain legible.
- `architecture_close`: mostly blank red panel with the flagpole at the right edge; low information but on target.
- `interior`: an exterior apron camera looking through the bay glass, not an interior position; appliances are visible so the role is weakly served.
- `loggia_close`: on target; the interior partition windows visible through the glass are a content issue (finding 13), not a camera issue.
