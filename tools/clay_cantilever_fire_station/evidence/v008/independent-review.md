# Independent holistic review: clay-cantilever-fire-station v008 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent, did not build. Evidence used: only `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution (plus enlarged crops), and the 4 `boards/*.png`. No code, reports or builder notes were read as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: total height and the loggia are now right, but the storey split is wrong. The glazed hall is about a third of the envelope height where the sources show about half, and the red box reads as two storeys. The tower is too short and too thin, and the loggia ceiling and the loggia roof slab carry the wrong finishes.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 4, P2 = 9
