# Independent holistic review — fire/v002 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent (did not build). Evidence: rendered pixels in `renders/*.png` (18 views) judged against locked `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg` only. Boards, metadata and builder notes were not used as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the massing, four-bay rhythm, SE glass clock tower and forecourt set are recognisably this station, but a black void at the SW cantilever/pier junction breaks the envelope, and the apparatus hall, east-end lobby, stepped lit soffit, loggia and tower proportion all deviate visibly from the locked sources.

## 4. Camera validity

No view is invalid. Notes:

- **interior** — the camera is outside on the apron looking at three closed bay doors; the appliances are legible only as red masses behind milky glass. The target (appliances visible through the bays) is weakly legible, not occluded; acceptable but a true interior or a clearer-glass pass would serve the role better.
- **tower_clock** — the red cap is clipped at the top edge by a few pixels; the clock and band (the targets) are fully in frame.
- **facade_close** — tower clipped at the right edge by design; bays and soffit edge fully legible.
- **loggia_close / architecture_close** — flagpole crosses the loggia and occludes one mullion; loggia depth and reveals remain readable.
- **front, top, aerial, front_corner, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side, glass_close, apparatus_bays, soffit_corner, apron_contact, rear_door** — target centred, unclipped, legible.
