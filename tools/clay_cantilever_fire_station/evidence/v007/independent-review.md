# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: clay cantilever fire station, v007

Reviewer: independent (no build code, reports, README or VERSIONS read). Evidence used: only `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution (plus upscaled crops of front_corner, front, loggia_close, soffit_corner, left_side and rear), and the 4 phone boards.

**Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 2, P2 = 8**

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The four v006 P1s are fixed or mostly fixed, but three blockers remain. The whole building is about half as tall as the sources show relative to its plan and bay rhythm, so it reads as a long low bar, not a heavy box. The south-west loggia is a ribbon window under a red head band, where the sources show a deep cut-out that reaches the parapet. The north sill band on the west face is a separate slab, proud of the wall with a seam (the v006 corner-block defect has moved north).
