## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the station is recognisable (red box over a glazed four-bay hall, SE glass clock tower now at the right height, east lobby and forecourt set), but a pure-black void persists at the hero SW corner, and the whole south-west grammar is rotated: the L-shaped leg drops on the south face instead of the west face, the loggia is a small window-like recess instead of the near-full-width deep west loggia, and the four bays occupy barely half of the south face instead of three-quarters.
