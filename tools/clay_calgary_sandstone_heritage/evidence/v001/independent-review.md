Reviewer: independent RLASM v6.1 holistic reviewer (not the builder). Judged only on rendered pixels against the locked sources `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I opened all 18 renders, the 3 sources and the 4 phone boards at full resolution. ## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The identity is legible: a square sandstone block round a court, four gabled corner pavilions under pyramids, a turreted clock tower with a copper spire in the middle of the east front, and the same storey tiers as the sources. It still has 10 clearly visible P1 defects. They fall into four groups: a roof plan that is too simple, flush pavilions, window rhythm that does not match the sources, and contact/opening voids.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 10, P2 = 11
