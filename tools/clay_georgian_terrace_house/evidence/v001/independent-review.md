# Independent holistic review: clay-georgian-terrace-house v001 (RLASM v6.1)

Reviewer: independent; no relationship with the builder. Basis: rendered pixels only (18 renders and 4 phone boards) against the locked `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I ignored the metadata, the hashes and the builder's notes. I made zoomed crops only in the new folder `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/georg-v001-indep-crops-r1/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**. The five-bay, three-storey and mansard massing reads correctly, but the left_side whole-envelope camera is invalid (the house is hidden behind an oversized neighbour box), and eleven visible fidelity and coherence defects remain. They are in the dormers, the flat arches, the first-floor sashes, the east-side windows, the chimney stacks, the upper roof, the rear plan, the rear wing and the neighbour terrace.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 11, P2 = 16
