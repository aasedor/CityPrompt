# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: Gastown heritage commercial block, clay v001

Reviewer: independent source-locked reviewer (not the builder). Basis: the three locked sources
(`sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`), the 18 renders in `renders/`, and the four
phone boards in `boards/`. I opened all of them at full resolution. I did not use metadata, counts, hashes or
builder notes as proof. I made zoomed crops in `review/crops_qzmvtk/` and sampled pixel values with a
read-only script. I changed no other file and rendered nothing.

## 1. Verdict

Verdict: FAIL

Counts: P0=0 P1=5 P2=9

The building is clearly the right one: a three-storey red-brick and buff-stone Gastown corner block with the
correct five-bay south front, the correct east-front rhythm, an arched top floor, a lettered frieze, a modillion
cornice, a crested pediment, shopfronts, a steam clock and a roof layout that matches the top view. It is still
not keeper-ready, so the lifecycle state is `visual_rework_required` (VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED). The blockers are:

- pure-black geometry slots in the east corner-shop jamb;
- a mis-assembled pediment, whose raking members overshoot and pass through the right pedestal;
- an undersized steam clock, placed too low and too close to the facade;
- three required baseline renders that are missing;
- side and rear cameras that are mostly blocked by neighbouring buildings.

## 4. Camera validity

- **front**: valid. The full south front and the visible east front are in frame. Margins are thin at the right edge (P2 #13).
- **front_corner**: valid as a matched oblique, and the target is legible. The bottom margin is thin at the clock base (P2 #13).
- **aerial**: valid, but the subject is small (about 25% of frame width) and the context boxes dominate (P2 #8).
- **top**: valid true top, with the whole footprint, roof furniture and street edges in frame. The dark west strip is shadowed lane ground, not a gap (P2 #6).
- **left_side**: **invalid as a whole-side proof.** The target is about 75% occluded by the west neighbours, and no grade or footprint of the subject is visible (P1 #5).
- **right_side**: valid. The full east elevation is in frame, with grade and cornice visible.
- **rear**: **invalid as a whole-rear proof.** The north neighbour box blocks the centre, and only the parapet band and edge strips are visible (P1 #5).
- **rear_side**: valid but weak. The subject is legible but small, and the across-street boxes partly hide the lower east facade.
- **Detail views** (steam_clock, storefronts, name_frieze_pediment, arched_windows, corner_pier, east_facade, cornice_brackets, roof_skylights, interior, rear_north): all legible and correctly targeted. rear_north is dominated by the neighbour roof in the foreground, but it shows the north party wall, coping, penthouse, chimney and the back of the pediment.
- **Missing**: facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close (P1 #4).
