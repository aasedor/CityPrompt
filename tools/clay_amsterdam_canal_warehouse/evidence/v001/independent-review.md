# Independent holistic review: canalwh v001 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent, adversarial. The only evidence used was the pixels of `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg`, `sources/top.jpg`, all 18 `renders/*.png` at full resolution, and the four phone boards. Zoomed crops were written only to `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/canalwh-v001-indep-crops/`.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The candidate is clearly the right building: four storeys, twin coped quay gables with pinnacles and hoist beams, stacked green loading doors over arched cart doors, an east wall with a cornice, a valley, a deck and two canals. There are no P0 defects, but nine P1 defects block approval. They cover the quay-gable composition, the plan proportions, the site scale, the roof-valley contact, the canal seam, the rear-face coherence, the glazing optics and the cameras.

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 9, P2 = 11

## 4. Camera validity

- **arched_doors: invalid.** The named target (the cart doors) is not in frame (F8).
- **front_corner and aerial: weak.** The building fills about 15–20 % of the frame width, so the details matched to the oblique source are illegible at native resolution (F9). The silhouette and plan are still readable.
- **top, right_side, rear, rear_side: legible but small** (building about 20–25 % of the frame). They are adequate for envelope and coherence checks only.
- **left_side: mostly occludes the target.** The neighbour and the party wall fill it. This is expected for the west party wall, but it is a low-value view (P2 F19).
- **front, facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, loading_doors, gable_tops, east_side, roof_deck, interior, rear_north: valid.** The target is in frame, unclipped and legible.
