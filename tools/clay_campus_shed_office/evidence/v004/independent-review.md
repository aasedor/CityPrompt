# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: clay campus shed office, v004

**Verdict: VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The v003 bay-order and canopy-span defects are fixed in the pixels. Four P1 defects remain: the southernmost sawtooth monitor sits north of the office box instead of over its window bay, the transom glazing band above the entrance canopy is missing, the south ground storey has the wrong material, and the "interior" camera misses its target. The canopy tie rod crossing the upper window is only partly fixed.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 5, P2 = 7**

Pixel coordinates are for the 1440x1080 renders unless noted. I judged every claim against the locked sources (front.png, oblique.jpg, top.jpg), not against builder notes.
