# Independent holistic review: The Reef (climbing_wall_building / variant_3), v007

Reviewer: independent RLASM v6.1 holistic reviewer. I opened all 18 renders at full resolution, the three locked sources and the four phone boards with the Read tool, and checked crops at 2-3x. Only rendered pixels were compared with locked source pixels. The builder's notes, metrics and counts were not accepted as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: the envelope, plan, skylight field, entrances, apron/curb and landscape all conform, but three clearly visible fidelity defects block production approval. The pockets read as glazed portholes or liquid-filled bowls. The roof plateau is grey-cyan and uncontoured where the source is a contour-banded seafoam roof. The walls are smooth ruled bulges without the source's sculpted relief.

Counts: **P0 = 0, P1 = 3, P2 = 7**
