# The Reef (climbing_wall_building / variant_3), v001: independent holistic review (RLASM v6.1)

Scope: I compared rendered pixels only against `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I opened all 18 renders and the 4 phone boards at full resolution and inspected magnified crops of the entrances, bays, domes, curb gaps, interior ceiling and source wall, entrance and dome areas. Pixel colours are sampled 2D averages. I did not use metadata or the builder's notes as evidence.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: The envelope, the four-lobe plan, the 12-dome layout, the apron with a four-gap curb and the ring path all match the sources. But the skylight domes, the cave-like entrances, the pocket finish and the white shell paths do not match their source identity, and the interior has clipped skylight wells and a boulder blocking a doorway. Together that is 6 P1 blockers.

## 4. Camera validity

- All 18 views are legible and the target is neither clipped nor hidden in any whole-envelope view.
- aerial, rear_side and architecture_close show the edge of the grey ground plane and the void behind it. This is a presentation issue (P2 #10), not an invalid camera.
- facade_close, climbing_pockets and meadow_path are close views that cut off part of the lobe at the frame edge. That is fine for detail views.
- interior is legible: the ceiling wells, boulders and floor are readable.
- No view is invalid.
