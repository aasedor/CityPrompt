# Independent holistic review: The Reef (climbing_wall_building / variant_3), candidate v005

Reviewer: independent (not the builder). Evidence: the three locked sources and all 18 renders, opened at full resolution, plus zoomed crops of the pocket, shoulder, interior ceiling, curb gap and hold regions. I did not use any builder metadata, counts or notes as proof.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The envelope, plan, bands, skylight field, entrances, apron, curb and landscape now conform. The climbing pockets are the source's signature wall texture, and they still do not read as the source's shallow, lit, rounded rock dishes: in close views they read as flat oval plugs under dark smudges, they crumple into faceted notches on the shoulder, and the faces no source shows are almost bare of them. The interior skylight openings also have no reveal.
