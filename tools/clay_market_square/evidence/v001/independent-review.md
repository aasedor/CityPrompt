# Independent holistic review: clay-market-square v001 (RLASM v6.1, architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent (did not build the candidate). Evidence used: rendered pixels only (18 PNGs in `renders/`, 4 phone boards) compared with the locked sources `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. I ignored metadata, counts, hashes and the builder's notes. I made zoomed crops in a new folder, `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-CityPrompt/18c9e177-8a96-5987-8fce-7444c75d018c/scratchpad/mktsq-review-crops-547ab675/`.

## 1. Verdict

**Verdict: VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**. The seating bowl's plan curvature is the reverse of the governing top view. The tiers bulge toward the plaza and narrow toward the stage, where the source arcs are concave to the plaza and widen as they rise. The bowl's risers, the bollards and the stage-flanking cheek walls also have source-visible defects that block approval.

Counts: P0 = 1, P1 = 3, P2 = 6

Previous-version findings: none. This is the first version (v001), so there are no earlier findings to carry forward or close.

| 1 | P0 | top, aerial, front_corner, cheek_walls, seating_tiers, meadow_beds, phone-01 board | **The bowl's curvature is reversed.** In all three sources the tier edges are concave to the plaza: they are ∩-shaped in top/oblique, the plaza pushes into the bowl at the centre line and the tier ends sweep south toward the viewer.
