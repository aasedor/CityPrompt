# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: plaza v004 (clay anchored plaza supermarket)

Reviewer: independent, no relationship with the builder. Inputs used: locked sources (front.png, oblique.jpg, top.jpg), all 18 renders at full resolution plus upscaled crops, and the 4 phone boards. No build code, reports or notes were read. Pixel coordinates are in the original render (1440x1080) unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The L-plan, the massing tiers and the shop-run grammar now read correctly. Three v003 items are fixed in the pixels. The anchor's defining green portal and sign band are still not the source composition, and the entrance is an empty recess. A new black intersecting element sits at the tower's east side, the west wing has an open slot against the anchor, and the pylon and two cameras are off.

**Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 7, P2 = 8**

## 4. Camera validity

- **anchor_entrance**: target missing. The tower and entrance are out of frame, so the view is invalid for its role (finding 6).
- **interior**: shows an exterior elevation, not an interior. It is invalid for its role (finding 6).
- **front**: the building is about 10% of the frame height at grade, so it is illegible on the phone board and not matched to the elevated source front (finding 7).
- left_side, right_side and rear are also very distant, low strips, but they serve faces no source shows and are legible at full resolution (acceptable).
- All other views (front_corner, aerial, top, rear_side, facade_close, architecture_close, glass_close, inline_shops, endcap_corner, parking_court, roof_units, rear_service) have their target unoccluded, unclipped and legible.
