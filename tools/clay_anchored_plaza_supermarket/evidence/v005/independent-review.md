# Independent RLASM v6.1 holistic review: plaza v005

Reviewer: independent (no build code, reports or builder notes read). Evidence: sources/front.png, sources/oblique.jpg, sources/top.jpg, all 18 renders at full resolution (1440x1080), the 4 phone boards, and enlarged crops of the matched areas.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED**: P0 = 0, P1 = 4, P2 = 9. The L-plan, the pylon, the closed west wing and the detail cameras are now right. Four visible fidelity defects remain: the tower's green hierarchy, the deep entrance niche, missing storefront walks with cars parked against the facade, and missing brick end and corner masses.
