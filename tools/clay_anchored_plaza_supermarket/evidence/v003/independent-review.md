# Independent holistic review — plaza v003 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent, no relationship with the builder. All three locked sources, all 18 renders and all four phone boards were opened at full resolution with the Read tool. Only rendered pixels were judged against locked source pixels; the build report, manifest and evidence folder were not used as proof. The v002 review was read only to know what the seven prior blockers were; each was re-verified in the v003 pixels.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — six of the seven v002 blockers are genuinely fixed in the pixels (the tower is now a volumetric box with brick-coloured piers, the cafe is two bays wide with no blank anchor wall, the canopy corner is closed, the sign no longer protrudes at the rear, the green vertical exists, the cameras fill the frame width), but a black sliver still runs the full height of the tower's south-west corner in every close view, and the west-end composition (tower mid-anchor with the sign band on its own face, three anchor bays between tower and cafe, no anchor tier behind the cafe) does not match the front, oblique or top source.
