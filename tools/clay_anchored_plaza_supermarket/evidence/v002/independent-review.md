# Independent holistic review — plaza v002 (RLASM v6.1 architectural clay)

Reviewer: independent, no relationship with the builder. Every render PNG (18), every board (4) and the three locked sources were opened at full resolution; zoomed crops were made of the whole-envelope views and the tower/corner regions to confirm pixel-level findings. Only rendered pixels were judged against locked source pixels.

## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED** — the L-plan, shop rhythm, continuous canopy, brick base, pilasters and pylon are all present and read correctly, but the anchor entrance tower (the primary identity element) is rendered as a thin parapet slab with pure-black faces and a sign panel that pokes out of its side, the cafe wing is far too narrow with a blank anchor wall where the source has storefront, the canopy mitre at the inner corner is open, and all eight whole-envelope cameras under-frame the asset to roughly 8–12 % of frame height.

## 4. Camera validity

- No view has its target occluded or the asset clipped.
- front, left_side, right_side, rear, rear_side, front_corner, aerial and top are valid but under-framed: the asset is 8–20 % of frame height, so facade detail is illegible at native resolution (finding 7). They should be re-framed before this package is used for the matched-role comparison.
- interior does not serve its role: it is a second exterior shot of the anchor entrance (finding 15).
- All ten detail views are legible and correctly targeted; endcap_corner shows the end-cap's south end rather than the inner L corner, which is where the canopy defect (finding 6) lives, so that defect is only visible in top and parking_court.
