Evidence used: only the rendered pixels in `renders/*.png` (all 18 opened at full resolution), the phone boards in `boards/*.png`, and the three locked sources `sources/front.png`, `sources/oblique.jpg` and `sources/top.jpg`. ## 1. Verdict

**VISUAL_REWORK_REQUIRED.** The candidate reads as a covered timber footbridge over a stream: it has a hipped grey standing-seam roof, transverse portal frames with knee braces, end-bay louvres, a glass balustrade, two round concrete piers and brick apartment context. Nine P1 defects block production approval. They are: a side-frame bracing rhythm that reads as a Warren/Howe truss instead of the source's portal frames with knee braces; louvres hanging ragged and unframed; a timber block piercing the roof at both hips; black voids on the portal post heads; an over-deep edge girder; no abutments, with the girder ends running into the bank; a pier cap that is misaligned and appears to float; and two camera problems (the front view is clipped, and a tree occludes the aerial).

Counts: P0 = 0, P1 = 9, P2 = 7
