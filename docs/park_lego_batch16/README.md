# Park LEGO Batch 16

Batch 16 closes the remaining variants for ten established park families:
community and regional parks, dog and skate parks, sports-field and tennis
clusters, botanical and Japanese gardens, memorial gardens, and urban forests.

The 29 new material sets are derived directly from their catalogue reference
images. Each variant keeps the measured, parcel-adaptive geometry of its
reviewed anchor family while receiving an explicit skin, planting structure,
and program marker. People remain scale references only; they are not emitted
by the LEGO assembly. Large buildings are excluded.

The comparison sheet pairs all forty catalogue cards with their six-role PBR
material palettes, including the eleven retained anchor identities.

City Prompt acceptance testing used one park at a time beside one separately
compiled building. Three tennis variants and the compact urban dog-park
variant compiled with their explicit family, appearance kit, and planting
structure. The live review also produced two reusable pipeline corrections:
source albedo must use a neutral material tint, and archetype-owned surface
families must suppress the older generic procedural diagram beneath their
metric LEGO assembly. The park polygon remains as the Google-tile replacement
mask, so surrounding photogrammetry cannot poke through the designed site.
