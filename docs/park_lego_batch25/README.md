# Park LEGO Batch 25 — Landscape-System Variant Closure

Batch 25 closes 30 previously unmapped variants across ten waterfront,
ravine, civic-square, topographic, post-industrial, picnic and watershed park
parents. Each selection stays on its proven executable family while receiving
an explicit appearance kit, planting structure, program component,
reference-derived six-role PBR pack and variant-aware 3D composition.

## Families

- Vancouver Seawall and Vancouver Beach Park
- Toronto Ravine and Toronto Urban Square
- Picturesque Olmsted Park
- Reclaimed Industrial Park
- Quarry Sunken Garden Park
- Hilltop Topographic Park
- Estate Picnic Grove
- Reservoir Watershed Park

The references govern site geometry as well as colour: waterfront edges stay
linear, ravine paths follow creek corridors, civic squares preserve their
central hardscape program, Olmsted variants retain their meadow/woodland/water
sequence, industrial relics remain distinct whole modules, quarry and hilltop
families build with stepped landform layers, picnic furniture repeats by
credible cluster, and reservoir programs keep water and perimeter movement
clear.

## Parcel adaptation

Oversized parcels add complete tree, table, pavilion, pier or relic modules;
compact parcels reduce counts. The builder does not stretch one park element
to fill the polygon. Landform, water and circulation hierarchies remain
recognizable at every supported size.

## Generation policy

- API image calls: **0**
- AI draping: **disabled**
- Source pixels projected into final ground: **no**
- People generated: **no**
- Embedded large buildings: **no**
- Generator: deterministic `park_kit`
