# RLASM v6.1 raw-photo forward standard — Amsterdam Bell Gable House

This package records the second complete raw-photo forward test of the
integrated RLASM v6.1 method. The building was constructed from three exact
catalogue photographs without a prebuilt model, sibling-variant mixing, or a
generic material fallback.

## Result

Candidate `10-amsterdam-bell-gable-house-rlasm-v10` received a holistic
independent decision of `PASS_ZERO_P0_ZERO_P1_KEEPER_APPROVED` on 2026-08-29.
The reviewer inspected the exact locked source set, all twenty full-resolution
renders, and both 1080-by-1920 phone boards without accepting builder metadata
as visual proof.

Together with the Barcelona Modernist Printing House keeper, this building is
a current RLASM v6.1 forward-standard exemplar.

The Blender and GLB binaries remain in the verified external artifact package
rather than ordinary Git history. Their reviewed SHA-256 values are recorded
in `keeper-manifest.json`.

## What the pilot proved

- Exact source pixels override conflicting family prose. The locked variant
  established a two-level rectangular wall body beneath the roof and a lower,
  continuous bell-gable hierarchy rather than an invented extra storey.
- A signature gable is construction and weathering topology. Its profile,
  scrolls, coping, crest, and hoisting beam must be source-measured, positively
  seated, and materially integrated rather than assembled from floating tubes
  or generic pale ornaments.
- Material fidelity includes morphology and scale. The final roof uses a fine,
  weathered Dutch-tile cadence, while the bell coping, scrolls, and compact
  crest remain one warm brick/terracotta family.
- A roof dormer requires a full-depth carrier cut, cheeks crossing the actual
  roof plane, a seated cap, turned-up side flashing, and a visible downslope
  apron—not merely a finished window placed on top of roof pixels.
- Residential identity includes circulation and occupation. The complete
  stair, landings, living/kitchen zone, and bedroom are visible through
  physical openings with returns, recessed sash, optical panes, and room
  depth.
- True top, whole-envelope margins, contact closes, interior proof, and phone
  comparison are all required; a successful front render is insufficient.

## Preserved iteration history

Versions v1 through v9 remain in the external pilot artifact with their failed
reviews. Each new version addressed a finite blocker list and rerendered the
complete evidence set. No failed candidate was overwritten or promoted by
editing metadata.

The principal corrections were: remove the extra wall storey; rebuild the
source box/shed dormer and full-depth roof cut; create a true orthographic top;
replace generic gable pipework with a continuous seated coping; expose dormer
flashing and connected stairs; correct roof-tile morphology and entrance
identity; smooth and resize the source scroll/crest geometry; and finally
replace the pale generic gable dressing with compact, continuously seated warm
terracotta construction.

## Package contents

- `build_candidate.py`, `prepare_candidate.py`, and `build_phone_boards.py`:
  reproducible construction, source-lock, and evidence-board scripts.
- `prework-manifest.json`, `discrepancy-ledger.json`, and
  `keeper-manifest.json`: measured contract, lifecycle, reviewed hashes, and
  promotion record.
- `references/asset-provenance.json`, all three exact catalogue sources, the
  locked source board, and the four exact-source material specimens used by
  the final model.
- `evidence/` and `review/`: builder evidence plus the decisive separate
  holistic review.
- `renders/`: all twenty full-resolution proof views.
- `phone/`: both reviewed phone-viewable comparison boards.

The canonical method is `docs/RLASM_LATEST_METHOD.md`; its executable companion
is `tools/archetype_compiler/rlasm_method.json`.
