# RLASM v6.1 raw-photo forward pilot — Barcelona Modernist Printing House

This package records the first full forward test of the integrated RLASM v6.1
method on a building that had only three catalogue archetype photographs. No
prebuilt building, sibling variant, or generic material fallback was used.

## Result

Candidate `07-barcelona-modernist-printing-house-rlasm-v7` received a holistic
independent decision of `PASS_ZERO_P0_ZERO_P1_KEEPER_APPROVED` on 2026-08-29.
The reviewer inspected the exact locked source set, all fourteen mandatory
full-resolution renders, and both 1080-by-1920 phone boards without accepting
builder metadata as visual proof.

The Blender and GLB binaries remain in the external artifact candidate rather
than ordinary Git history. The reviewed GLB SHA-256 is
`2385CB7C1085DA62028E9A1FA7A2D9B0E1864C7684270B3222FA5B454BEDBDAB`.

## What the pilot proved

- Exact front, oblique, and top catalogue pixels can drive a new building from
  source lock through measured plan, roof, bay, material, opening, contact,
  program, camera, phone-board, and independent-review gates.
- A multi-wing transparent monitor must be one sealed weathering union with
  one source-conditioned optical authority across every facet, continuous
  curb/flashing, bounded frame nodes, and a full-depth roof aperture.
- Roof-edge consoles, iron lattice, finials, shoulder reliefs, and crests are
  source-bearing architecture, not interchangeable decorative trim.
- Transparent architecture needs a recognizable occupied workflow. The platen
  presses, paper handling, type storage, work surfaces, circulation, and room
  depth are visible in both close and whole-envelope evidence.
- Whole-envelope cameras require visible neutral safety margin beyond the
  complete grade, footprint, and highest termination.

## Preserved iteration history

Versions v1 through v6 remain in the external pilot artifact with their failed
reviews. Each new version addressed only the finite blocker list and rerendered
the complete evidence set. Nothing was overwritten or converted into a pass by
editing metadata.

The principal corrections were: rebuild the roof monitor as a joined T;
remove invented white-stone ribbons; repair brick scale and curved-surface
mapping; create physical recessed openings; make the printing workflow
unmistakable; replace overlapping monitor facets with a sealed manifold;
correct camera safety margins; and finally rebuild the console, fine diamond
ironwork, finial, relief, and elevation-specific crest grammar from the locked
sources.

## Package contents

- `build_candidate.py` and `build_phone_boards.py`: reproducible construction
  and evidence-board scripts.
- `prework-manifest.json`, `discrepancy-ledger.json`, and
  `references/asset-provenance.json`: source lock, measured contract, lifecycle,
  and derivative provenance.
- `evidence/`: final machine-readable builder evidence.
- `review/`: separate builder and holistic independent review records.
- `renders/`: selected full-resolution proof views.
- `phone/`: the two reviewed phone-viewable comparison boards.

The canonical method is `docs/RLASM_LATEST_METHOD.md`; its executable companion
is `tools/archetype_compiler/rlasm_method.json`.
