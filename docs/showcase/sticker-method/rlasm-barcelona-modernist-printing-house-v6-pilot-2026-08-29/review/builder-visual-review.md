# Builder visual review — Barcelona Modernist Printing House v7

Status: `BUILDER_PASS_COMPLETE`

The builder review did not promote this candidate. The separate holistic
independent review subsequently approved it with zero P0 and zero P1 blockers;
that decision is recorded in `independent-visual-review.md` and
`independent-visual-review.json`.

This is a raw-photo pilot built from the three locked `barcelona_modernist_workshop` variant-2 catalogue images. No prebuilt building, sibling variant, or generic material fallback was used.

## Source-locked checks

- Front identity: three bays, black iron post-and-beam grid, warm brick upper infill, arched upper windows, full-height ground glazing, central entrance, lobed parapet, circular printing rosette.
- Plan and roof: rectangular 15 m x 18 m envelope, four side bays, flat weathering plane, four parapet centers, corner pylons, iron X railings, and a T-shaped glazed roof lantern.
- Envelope continuity: the same structural, brick, opening, plinth, and parapet grammar continues across front, both sides, and rear.
- Program: printing presses, type cabinets, worktables, stair, two occupied floors, and restrained warm room lighting are visible through the glazing.

## Physical checks

- Upper openings are cut through their wall panels and contain brick returns, inset blackened-iron frames, neutral glass, a separately recessed occupied layer, and room sidewalls.
- Ground glazing bears on a dark steel base beam; columns terminate at limited warm-gray stone feet; parapet and lantern components have explicit supports and contacts.
- The registered printing rosette is mounted on a matching physical brick carrier at the audited exterior datum.
- The roof lantern is one joined T-shaped glass envelope with closed vertical faces, a full-depth roof aperture, explicit interior roof returns, one continuous dark curb/flashing outline, bounded glass facets, and ridges seated into closed end frames.

## Evidence checks

- All fourteen required named renders exist and are readable, including low-oblique lantern contact and printing-press program proof.
- Whole-envelope front, side, rear, oblique, and aerial views retain grade-to-parapet framing with margin.
- Phone boards compare exact locked source roles with matched model roles and expose identity, opening depth, roof contact, and visible program.
- Machine preflight passes with zero deterministic failures; it is not treated as visual approval.

## Builder severity decision

No P0 or P1 blocker was found in the final builder pixel scan. This is not a keeper decision. A separate reviewer must inspect the locked source board and every mandatory render without relying on this review or metadata.

## Bounded v1-to-v7 corrections

- Rebuilt the monitor as one source-ratio T envelope instead of overlapping shed pieces.
- Replaced square capped corner pylons with crossed lobed brick finials; reduced railing section/height; thinned the roof cornice; increased small corbel cadence.
- Removed continuous white stone from plinths, upper sills, corner caps, and lantern curbs; stone remains only at measured structural feet and paper/curb support roles.
- Replaced axis-only brick projection with reference-conditioned tri-planar mapping and reduced saturation/course scale to match the tawny source.
- Increased glazing transmission and neutral QA exposure while preserving pane, reveal, occupied-depth, and room offsets.
- Added complete platen presses with bed, platen, rollers, paper feed, type tray, and flywheel; separated them from generic workshop furniture and framed dedicated proof.
- Replaced the first joined-but-shallow monitor with a taller, closed T monitor and removed redundant valley rods; the interior-up view now proves the actual roof cut, returns, glazing, and daylight.
- Replaced the registered brick-and-iron rosette with an iron-only, true-alpha derivative so the physical envelope brick remains the sole brick authority.
- Replaced the clean modular roof sheet with an exact-source-conditioned aged bituminous membrane containing broad irregular repair patches and laps.
- Replaced dithered optical layers with blended transmission and re-authored the program camera through a clear pane.
- Replaced the visually overlapping v4 monitor facets with one shared-vertex glazed T envelope, a single joined flashing skirt, a single curb/roof-return mesh, capped square frame members, bounded valleys, and an explicit central node.
- Pulled aerial and rear-side cameras back to preserve the complete visible footprint and neutral grade margin.
- Reduced the registered rosette and carrier to the locked source proportion; replaced stepped ziggurat corner forms with compact lobed brick pinnacles.
- Replaced detached cuboid dentils with shaped brick brackets that overlap and physically support the cornice datum.
- Moved printing evidence inside the clear room volume: the close now shows the complete platen, bed, rollers, paper feed, flywheel, base, and adjacent work surface without a facade mullion or specular wash.
- Replaced the shared window-glass response on the roof monitor with one exact-source-calibrated aged translucent monitor material across every vertical, end, valley, and sloped facet, eliminating the perforated/opaque checker read while retaining bounded reflection.
- Enlarged front-corner and aerial safety margins so the complete visible footprint, grade, parapet, and monitor remain inside the frame.
- Rebuilt all corner finials as taller faceted pointed brick pinnacles from the locked 60-degree source and refined the cornice support cadence into slender tapered brackets with positive contact at the cornice datum.
- Replaced the flat bracket tabs with true projecting 3D console profiles: each has a lower wall seat, narrowed neck, and broad head in positive contact with the cornice underside.
- Rebuilt the coarse single-row X railing as two stacked rows of fine, dense diamond lattice with thinner rails, posts, and diagonals at the locked source cadence.
- Differentiated front, rear, left, and right crest silhouettes and added bounded triangular shoulder relief geometry around the rosettes instead of repeating one smooth generic crest template.
