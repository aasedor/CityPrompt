# Highway motor hotel — catalogue-conditioned RLASM pilot

This is the first building produced with the tightened RLASM gate: the actual
catalogue archetype images were supplied to reference generation, the source
hashes were locked before construction, and the model received a full
geometry and architectural review against the hero, aerial and side views.

**Review state:** builder review passed; independent approval pending. This
candidate is **not yet a keeper**.

## Gate result

- Exact archetype contract: one-storey U-shaped motor court with two room
  wings, a rear cross-wing, an open road entry, embedded front office,
  repeated exterior room access, shallow roofs and a two-post pylon sign.
- Physical construction: 558 scene objects, including the U-envelope, closed
  roof solids and valley closures, doors, paired windows, PTAC units, battens,
  parking geometry, office and pylon.
- Material gate: zero facade stickers and zero generic material fallbacks.
  Source crops remain provenance evidence only; source-calibrated procedural
  construction materials are used because the oblique crops were not
  seam-safe.
- Required review views passed at builder level, including the topological
  aerial and the rear-oblique roof-junction proof.
- The full findings and pending approval state are recorded in
  `geometry-architectural-review.json`; source roles and hashes are recorded
  in `prework-manifest.json`.
