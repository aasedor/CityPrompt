# Victorian Grand Station — inhabited source-locked reconstruction

This package records the bounded RLASM v5 rebuild of the Victorian iron-and-
glass grand station. V12 was rejected because it interpreted the family label
as a shallow open barrel cage behind a generic brick strip. V16 corrected the
outer architectural type but was also rejected because its transparent shed
exposed an almost empty floor. V18 reconstructs both the envelope and the
working station visible beneath it.

## What changed

- The shed is now a long continuous enclosed glass vault, not sparse ribs.
- Physical transverse trusses, purlins, glazed end frames, side columns and
  arches establish the source load path.
- The masonry frontage is low and asymmetrical, with differentiated gables and
  one offset clock tower.
- Beneath the vault are two complete track beds with sleepers and paired rails,
  three raised platforms, supported glass canopies, benches, a guarded
  pedestrian gallery, connected stairs and an interior concourse arcade.
- Dominant surfaces use exact-source Victorian brick, sandstone and slate at
  measured physical scales.
- The oblique clock crop was replaced by a source-conditioned orthographic
  identity. Its prompt, hashes and registration contract are preserved in
  [the provenance record](station-orthographic-clock-sticker-provenance.json).

## Status

V18 passes the source-locked builder review with 765 objects, 13 review
renders, two registered clock identities and zero generic fallbacks. It remains
a candidate—not a keeper—until a separate reviewer records zero P0 blockers.
