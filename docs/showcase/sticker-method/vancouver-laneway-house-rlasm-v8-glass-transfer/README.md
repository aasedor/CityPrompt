# Vancouver Laneway House — RLASM low-iron glass transfer

This is the second-archetype transfer test of the Toronto-approved
`rlasm_heritage_low_iron_bluegrey_v1` module.

The Vancouver candidate preserves its own RLASM prework, pale vertical cedar,
warm entry cedar, black frames, glass balcony, standing-seam hip roof, and
opening-card hierarchy. The shared element is the abstract low-iron reflection
card and physical glass shader.

Important transfer finding: the inherited Vancouver walls are not physically
cut behind every side window. Moving the room cards to the Toronto-certified
0.16 m depth caused siding to appear through the panes. Candidate v8 therefore
uses the sealed 0.03 m legacy surface-assembly depth and records that it is not
full-depth certification. RLASM now requires a real aperture, returns, and
opaque backing before a 0.16 m room-card depth may be claimed.
