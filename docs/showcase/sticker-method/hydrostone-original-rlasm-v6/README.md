# Hydrostone Original — Iconic Tudor RLASM 1.7.0 v6

Hydrostone v6 is the accepted non-overwriting rebuild produced after the user correctly rejected v4 as insufficiently Tudor. The correction was structural rather than cosmetic: the building now uses Tudor massing, depth, roof and window grammar instead of applying half-timber trim to a rectangular shell.

## Tudor identity reconstruction

- One dominant transverse roof intersects four unequal, shallow cross-gables.
- The full upper storey physically jetties 0.46 m beyond the brick base and rests on bearers and corbels.
- Main, central, left and right gables differ in height, width, projection and aperture treatment.
- Eleven front apertures—including two gable-peak windows—and four side apertures are physically cut.
- Upper openings are tall, grouped and projected with bay heads, cheeks and aprons.
- Fine physical diamond leadwork replaces the rejected oversized zigzag pattern.
- Timber rails, opening-edge studs and short braces frame solid panels rather than crossing windows.
- The long side wing continues the Tudor panel rhythm instead of becoming a generic white wall.
- The narrow entry gable clears the stair-bay window and contains a recessed paneled oak door.
