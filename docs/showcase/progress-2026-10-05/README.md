A free local bicycle preview follows the drawn route with a level camera 1.6 m above the resolved surface. This 16.3 m pilot downloaded successfully as an eight-second, 192-frame, 720p H.264 video. Finished generation is disabled in this classroom trial. Capture takes minutes in this session; sparse park detail and distorted Google context at ground level remain visible limitations.
