# Final image prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation, one edit call per clay guide.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: sketch-to-render
Asset type: architectural feasibility render for the City Prompt semantic-clay trial
Input images: Image 1 is the exact architectural-clay edit target and geometry authority.
Primary request: turn this exact Calgary inner-city Craftsman bungalow clay model into a high-quality photorealistic exterior architectural rendering by adding realistic materials, site context, lighting, and atmosphere only.
Scene/backdrop: a proportionally sized established Calgary residential lot with simple prairie-tolerant planting, lawn, and a short front walk; quiet unobtrusive background.
Style/medium: photorealistic architectural visualization, credible real-world construction, natural camera response.
Composition/framing: preserve the input camera, front three-quarter viewpoint, crop, footprint, and one-and-a-half-storey scale; keep the complete building visible.
Lighting/mood: clear soft late-afternoon daylight, natural shadows, calm and lived-in rather than glossy luxury marketing.
Materials/textures: warm variegated red-brown brick on the muted masonry zones; pale cream mineral stucco or painted siding on light wall zones; charcoal asphalt shingles; painted off-white wood trim; stained cedar porch posts and rails; clear neutral low-e glazing with subtle interior depth; realistic concrete steps and foundation.
Constraints: geometry fidelity is the primary test. Preserve the exact silhouette, roof hierarchy, porch, front gable, dormers, chimney, steps, wall proportions, window and door count, window and door locations, and every opening rhythm from Image 1. Change surfaces and setting only. No stretched facade, no added or removed bays, no extra floor, no redesigned roof, no moved entrance, no invented garage, no people, no vehicles, no text, no logo, no watermark.
Avoid: changing the building design; exaggerated wide-angle distortion; fantasy landscaping; generic modern farmhouse redesign; over-saturated HDR.
```

## Per-variant additions

- Native: preserve the native footprint and camera exactly.
- Medium: preserve the +2-whole-bay left wing; do not compress it to native.
- Wide: preserve all +4 whole side bays and both end conditions; the facade
  must remain substantially wider than both other variants.
