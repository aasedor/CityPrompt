# Sticker Method entrance-and-roof pilot

This review package records the focused Catalan Modernista landmark pilot. The asset retains the Sticker Method for exact façade identity and is approved only as a predetermined **select-and-place landmark**.

It is not polygon-stretchable. Translation and rotation are allowed; non-uniform scaling and floor-count changes are forbidden. The authored footprint is **14.0 m × 31.0 m**, with a nominal visible height of **24.6 m**.

## Result

- Architect review: **86/100 — approve with notes** (release threshold: 85).
- Machine validation: **pass**.
- Surface-finish/parity audit: **pass**, foreground similarity **0.99874**.
- Paid image/API calls for this focused reconstruction: **0**.

## What changed

- The high-fidelity elevation remains a continuous registered sticker; projected balcony/oriel stickers use the same metre-to-UV frame.
- The left entrance sticker is cut on the actual round-arch profile instead of a rectangular hole.
- The doorway is a three-metre physical recessed passage with masonry reveals and a framed gate at the back.
- The roof is a closed, transverse asymmetric dragon-back shell with constructed skirts down to the wall plate.
- The left turret, central triple pots, and paired right chimney stacks are fixed skyline assemblies.
- Side and rear sticker elevations remain authored, with duplicate physical window/balcony overlays removed.

## Approved limitations

The remaining weakness is roof material fidelity: the tiles are still more uniformly brown and rectangular than the archetype’s multicoloured glazed scales. The left turret is somewhat oversized/central, the front roof-wall junction is flatter than the reference, and secondary elevations remain more repetitive than the landmark front. These are refinement notes, not release blockers for the fixed landmark pilot.
