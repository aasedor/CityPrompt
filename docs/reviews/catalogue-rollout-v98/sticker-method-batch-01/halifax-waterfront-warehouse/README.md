# Halifax Waterfront Warehouse - Sticker Method V98.4

Building 4 of Sticker Method Batch 01 is approved for two bounded horizontal
LEGO tiers. The 20 x 15 m canonical tier scored 95.45 and the 25 x 15 m
extended tier scored 95.35, for an exact family mean of 95.40. The independent
architect found zero hard stops.

The exact three-view reference set overrides conflicting catalogue prose. It
locks a two-storey warm-brick Italianate commercial block, pale rusticated
stone frame, deep green storefronts, physical round-arched upper windows,
layered patinated cornice, raised front crown, flat membrane roof, and two
independently capped masonry chimneys.

## Corrections that made the transfer pass

Every arched glass and interior carrier is explicitly clipped to the same
approved contour as its physical opening. The upper brick closures are closed,
outward-wound, non-overlapping solids; an earlier double shell that produced
black Cycles panels was removed. Straight coping runs meet four closed corner
caps without intersection. Stone and brick use shared metric UV scale, glass
uses a reflective physical material in front of separate subdued interiors,
and all arches, returns, frames, transoms, doors, cornice layers and modillions
remain geometry-owned.

The canonical export audited 7,620 visible faces and the extended export 8,804,
all owned exactly once with zero generic fallbacks. Both GLB families passed
structural validation and full nine-view Cycles review.

## LEGO contract

The extended tier inserts two complete 2.5 m ordinary bays: the front changes
from three bays plus the fixed central pavilion plus three bays to four plus the
fixed pavilion plus four. It does not stretch brick, arches, quoins, cornice,
roof, depth or height; it does not add an entrance, crown or chimney.

## Known bounded limitations

Glazing remains darker and more repetitive than the reference, the central
ground entrance reads somewhat opaque, the roof membrane has restrained rather
than pronounced patch weathering, and the pale stone relief is soft. These are
scored deductions, not release blockers.
