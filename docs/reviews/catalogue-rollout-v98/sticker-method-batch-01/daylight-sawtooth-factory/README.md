LEGO tiers. The canonical six-tooth tier scored 97.0 and the extended

## What made the transfer pass

Locked geometry owns every tooth, gable, opening return, physical sash bar,
northlight grid, dock edge and hollow chimney ring. Intrinsic stickers own
brick, slate, concrete, steel and glass identity at real-world scale. Wall
glass is a transmissive industrial material in front of separately recessed
4×2 workshop atlas cells; neither frames nor false depth are printed into the
glass. Each roof tooth receives a deterministic optical phase so weathering
does not stamp the same mark across the roof.

The asset preparation step derives optical-atlas thirds from the real source
dimensions, preventing out-of-bounds black padding. The canonical export
audited 6,135 faces and the extended export 7,531 faces, with zero ownership
failures. Both GLB families passed structural validation and production
preflight.

## Known bounded limitations

Northlights are somewhat darker than the sunlit exact references, roof
weathering remains mildly regular under a strict top-down audit, and the
finest brick corbels and dock weathering are simplified. The rear is a
constrained completion from exact oblique and aerial evidence, not an exact
rear claim. These are scored deductions rather than release blockers.
