# Old Montreal Textile Mill — Sticker Method V98.1

Building 1 of Sticker Method Batch 01 is approved for two bounded horizontal
LEGO tiers. The canonical five-bay tier scored 96.0 and the extended seven-bay
tier scored 95.5, for an exact family mean of 95.75. The independent architect
found zero hard stops.

The exact images, rather than conflicting four-to-six-storey metadata, lock
this archetype as a three-storey adaptive-reuse textile mill. Its scalable
capacity is horizontal only: complete 5 m structural bays may be inserted, but
floors, partial bays, non-uniform stretching, and duplication of the singular
corner/service identity are forbidden.

## What made the transfer pass

Geometry owns every silhouette, arch, reveal, sill, cornice, monitor and true
opening. Intrinsic stickers own brick, limestone, gravel and occupied interior
identity at constant world scale. Physical glazing sits in front of recessed,
floor-specific interior cards; it does not print frames or false depth. Five
non-adjacent interior variants per floor prevent obvious repeated rooms.

The canonical export audited 5,943 faces and the extended export 7,139 faces,
with zero ownership failures. Both GLB families passed structural validation.

## Known bounded limitations

The rear is a constrained completion from the exact oblique and aerial images,
not an exact rear claim. HVAC and the rear service composition are simplified,
and interiors are recessed cards rather than complete modeled rooms. These were
scored deductions, not release blockers.
