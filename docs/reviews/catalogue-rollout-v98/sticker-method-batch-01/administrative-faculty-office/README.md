## Corrections that made the transfer pass

The projection's front infill is warm brick while its explicit perimeter,
spandrel bands and solid cheek remain weathered bronze. Ordinary side/rear
floor joints use subdued brick rather than the pale entrance finish. The entry
has physical pale reveals, a fritted curtain-glass sticker, a 1.8 m open lobby
tunnel and doors at the back. Physical glass sits in front of neutral-veiled,
deterministically varied office cards. Asset publishing is atomic, and Blender
fully decodes each registered sticker before any render can proceed.

The canonical export audited 5,770 faces and the extended export 6,730 faces,
with zero ownership failures. Both GLB families passed structural validation.
An explicit regression test prevents extended rear bays or their interior
cards from crossing the locked 37.5 m footprint.

## Known bounded limitations

Glazing remains dark in some views, bronze can read brown in shadow, the
constrained rear is generic, and the mechanical court is more orderly than the
exact reference. These were scored deductions, not release blockers.
