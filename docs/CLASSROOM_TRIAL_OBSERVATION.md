# First classroom trial — instructor sheet

Use [Your first neighbourhood](CLASSROOM_STUDENT_EXERCISE.md) with one or two
students who have not used the prototype. A 35-minute task plus a short debrief
is enough for the first round. This sheet is a trial protocol, not a completed
student assessment. Agent rehearsals do not establish independent usability.

**Scheduling update:** students are unavailable until winter term. In the
meantime, use documented agent simulations through the ordinary UI as provisional
prototype checkpoints. Record retries and assistance, and continue bounded
development; do not hold all catalogue work until winter. Retain this sheet for
the actual student trial when class begins. See
[the September simulation](SIMULATED_STUDENT_EXERCISE_2026-09-22.md).

## Before the session

- Provide a working project URL and login on the available classroom/test
  machine. The current QA service is local; students on other machines cannot
  use someone else's `127.0.0.1` URL. Broader hosting/account rollout is separate.
- Give each participant their own empty project. Keep the completed Currie
  examples intact. Point out the vacant parcel between the straight east road
  and curving west road; the student should trace its boundary.
- If testing only the design/presentation stages, supply a blank prepared parcel
  and mark site setup **provided**, rather than calling it independently passed.
- Use the handout's existing detailed assets. No new catalogue activation or
  paid API calls are required. Keep custom image generation off for this round.
- Check Google context and catalogue cards load, then let the student begin.

## Observe without coaching first

Ask the student to say what they expect to happen. When stuck, ask “What are you
trying to do?” before explaining a control. Give help when needed and record it;
the goal is useful evidence, not a stressful test. Do not count instructor fixes
as independent success.

## Debrief — 5 minutes

1. What was easiest? What was most frustrating?
2. What did you expect to snap or connect automatically?
3. Which building, street or park did you want but could not find?
4. Would you trust the downloaded image to explain your idea?

Keep the saved project, final PNG and short observations together. Avoid storing
passwords or unnecessary personal details in reports.

## Decide the next batch

Fix lost work, missing/buried models, unusable controls and misleading exports
before another session. Prioritize repeated confusion that required help. Small
tile seams and construction detailing are follow-up items unless they prevent
the student from communicating the design.

For an actual student trial, look for students creating, editing, recovering,
reopening and presenting a plausible scene without step-by-step coaching. Until
winter, a recorded UI simulation can support a provisional small catalogue batch;
label it accordingly and choose gaps from the rehearsal and instructor priorities.
Once available, use student requests above. Complete the shared runtime review
per exact variant; a new card alone is not an integrated classroom asset.
