# Park LEGO Batch 20

Batch 20 adds explicit catalogue coverage for thirty variants across ten
ecology, recreation and civic-space parents: riparian buffers, wetland rain
gardens, adventure playgrounds, amphitheatre lawns, beer gardens, city-hall
forecourts, sunken plazas, cathedral forecourts, cultural-institution
forecourts and stepped plazas.

Every new selection retains the parcel-adaptive whole-program grammar of its
reviewed family while receiving an explicit appearance kit, planting/program
identity and six-role PBR skin derived from that variant's own catalogue
reference. The comparison sheet includes all four variants per parent so the
new skin can be reviewed beside both newly mapped and retained anchor images.
People in the references inform scale only; the LEGO output contains no people,
AI drape or embedded large buildings.

The one-park-at-a-time City Prompt trial used Beer Garden / Brewery Tap Yard on
a 39.7 m by 31.2 m polygon beside a separately compiled Parisian Boulevard
building. The site remained a flat planning polygon until Generate to 3D, then
persisted the exact Batch 20 variant skin and generated whole trestle modules,
a regular shade-tree grid, a bounded park-serving kiosk and overhead strings.

Live validation exposed a source-tile contact failure in which independently
sampled ground vertices struck incompatible Google photogrammetry heights and
pulled the park surface into two tent-like triangles. Compiled parks now use
one robust sampled elevation as a level prepared datum, and all LEGO kit parts
seat on the same datum. Streets continue to follow corridor grades. The fixed
trial result is a stable, level park surface that masks the source tiles below
without deforming toward the adjacent building.
