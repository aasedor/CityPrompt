## Student card drafts

**Prairie School house** — detached single-family use; Prairie School style.
Prairie School houses use low roofs, broad eaves and bands of windows to
emphasize the horizon. Rooms often connect more openly to one another and to
the landscape. Look for a low hip roof, horizontal bands and a broad chimney.
Main idea: connect domestic space to the landscape.

The history and organic-architecture association are grounded in the
[Frank Lloyd Wright Trust](https://www.flwright.org/explore/prairie-style).
The candidate is an original generated design, not a model of a named Wright
building. Its claimed style is independent from the generic word Prairie.

**Postmodern neighbourhood library** — civic/library use; Postmodern style.
Postmodern architecture mixes familiar historical forms with modern materials,
colour and playful changes of scale. Buildings may use oversized pediments,
contrasting shapes or symbolic entrances to communicate with people and respond
to their surroundings. Main idea: make architecture expressive and readable.

This interpretation follows [Historic England's introduction to Post-Modern
architecture](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/iha-post-modern-architecture/).
These are design associations, not mandatory beliefs or guarantees about social
or environmental performance.

## Delivery boundary

Use the canonical RLASM 6.1 architectural-clay profile. Each new original
reference board is locked before modelling; complete GLB export/reimport renders
and an independent holistic review are required. Keep fixed native dimensions,
geometry-led openings, complete elevations and source-specific silhouettes.
No full rectangular ground pad is exported. QA context is green and excluded
from the GLB; site landscaping belongs to the shared runtime.

Offline visual acceptance, runtime integration and human activation are separate
decisions. New candidates remain outside the student seed/runtime catalogue.
Every candidate carries the unrun runtime template and measured entrance notes;
offline evidence cannot claim placement, save/reload or terrain tests passed.
