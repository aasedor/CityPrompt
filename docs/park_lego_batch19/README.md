# Park LEGO Batch 19

Batch 19 closes all four variants for ten destination, seasonal, waterfront,
and cycling park families: outdoor ice rinks, kayak launches, tidal-marsh
boardwalks, outdoor cinemas, food-truck plazas, festival lawns, campus quads,
urban beaches, velodromes, and mountain-bike parks.

Each variant retains the parcel-adaptive whole-program grammar of its reviewed
anchor while receiving an explicit appearance kit, planting/program identity,
and six-role PBR skin derived from that variant's own catalogue reference. The
renderer preserves distinctive three-dimensional kit parts and neutralizes
material tint so the reference-derived albedo remains visible. People in the
references inform scale only; LEGO output contains no people, AI drape, or
embedded large buildings.

The comparison sheet pairs all forty catalogue cards with their material-role
palettes, including the ten retained anchor variants. The one-park-at-a-time
City Prompt trial used the compact Industrial-Lot Pop-Up food-truck variant on
a 39.7 m by 31.2 m polygon beside a separately compiled Parisian Boulevard
building. It remained a flat colored polygon until Generate to 3D, then
persisted the exact family and skin with six parcel-fitted truck volumes,
communal tables, string lights, planting, and zero AI-drape calls. The trial
also established a dedicated 500-1,500 m2 compatibility envelope for this
compact variant rather than inheriting the larger permanent park minimum.
