# Park object kits P0 v1

This bounded catalogue closes the highest-priority 3D-object gaps found by the
50-call park composition study. Ground, court, planting and safety surfaces
remain owned by the archetype-derived LEGO skin system; the 25 GLBs add only
metric depth-bearing objects. No people or large buildings are generated.

## Families

- Disc golf: regulation basket and tee sign.
- Bocce / petanque: score stand and ball rack.
- Climbing / bouldering: competition wall, natural boulder and traverse wall.
- Mini golf: windmill, loop, bridge and cup flag.
- Beach volleyball: competition net and referee stand.
- Water / ecology: boardwalk, observation deck, footbridge, weir, inlet,
  outlet, riprap and maintenance gate.
- Amphitheatre: stage, repeatable seat wall, aisle rail and bollard light.

The Blender generator and machine-readable source are in
`tools/park_skin_compiler/`. `validation-report.json` verifies every manifest,
GLB header, catalogue flag and expected file. The family sheets are visual QA
views; the two catalogue sheets show representative assets from both angles.
