# Park LEGO Batch 24 — City Park Variant Closure

Batch 24 closes 30 previously unmapped variants across ten Calgary, Halifax,
London, Montreal, and New York park parents. Each selection remains on its
existing proven procedural family but now has an explicit appearance kit,
planting structure, program component, reference-derived six-role PBR pack,
and variant-aware 3D composition.

## Families

- Calgary Prairie Urban Plaza
- Calgary Prince's Island Park
- Halifax Coastal Forest Park
- Halifax Public Gardens
- London Circus
- London Garden Square
- Montreal Mount Royal Park
- Montreal Neighbourhood Square
- New York Community Garden
- New York Pocket Park

The comparison sheet shows the exact catalogue render used for each new pack
and the six compiled material roles. People in the catalogue images were used
only to understand feature scale and use. They are not rendered. Surrounding
or enclosing large buildings are also excluded and remain separate building
LEGO families. Small park-owned structures such as bandstands, pergolas,
garden sheds, and overlook decks remain valid kit elements.

## Parcel adaptation

The shared families now branch on the exact variant instead of repeating the
anchor recipe. Oversized parcels increase counts of complete beds, planter
bays, trees, and benches. Compact parcels reduce those counts while retaining
the reference hierarchy and regulation-scale circulation. Nothing stretches a
single element beyond a credible park module simply to fill a polygon.

## Generation policy

- API image calls: **0**
- AI draping: **disabled**
- Source pixels projected into final ground: **no**
- People generated: **no**
- Embedded large buildings: **no**
- Generator: deterministic `park_kit`
