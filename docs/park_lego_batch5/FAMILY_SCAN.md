# Park-family catalogue scan - Batch 5 selection

The scan compared the 130 open-space archetypes against the executable park
families through Batch 4. Candidates were ranked on five criteria: distinct
spatial grammar, reference clarity, reusable 3D-kit value, ability to adapt to
irregular polygons without distorting a regulation element, and independence
from a separately rendered large building.

## Selected now

| Rank | Exact v0 family | Why it adds useful methodology |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Beach Volleyball / Competition | Tests a complete regulation sand/free-zone envelope and net/referee kit |
| 2 | Bocce / Italian Piazza | Tests a narrow regulation lane plus a social/pergola edge |
| 3 | Climbing / Competition Boulder | Adds a reusable angular wall, holds and impact-mat vocabulary |
| 4 | Disc Golf / Wooded Championship | Tests paired tee/target logic and non-crossing corridors across a large irregular park |
| 5 | Mini Golf / Classic Themed | Tests repeated small lanes, ordered circulation and distinct obstacle kit |
| 6 | Bioswale / Streetside | Tests linear hydraulic continuity, inlets, crossings and check dams |
| 7 | Urban Orchard / Heritage Apple | Tests adding/removing whole tree rows while preserving working access |
| 8 | Pollinator Meadow / Prairie Restoration | Tests non-monochrome habitat drifts and low vertical microdetail |
| 9 | Sculpture Garden / Museum Court | Adds reusable plinth and abstract-sculpture modules with viewing clearances |
| 10 | Labyrinth / Classical Stone | Tests a topology-critical concentric route that cannot be generically approximated |

## Recommended next queue

1. Outdoor Ice Rink / Multipurpose Pad - valuable seasonal-state and boards kit.
2. Kayak Launch / River Launch - reusable floating dock, rack and launch-slide kit.
3. Tidal Marsh Boardwalk / Cordgrass - long boardwalk and overlook logic distinct
   from the smaller wetland family.
4. Outdoor Cinema / Park Lawn - screen, projection booth and audience-sightline
   grammar without rendering spectators.
5. Food-Truck Plaza / Permanent Park - utility bays and furniture layout; trucks
   should be a shared prop family.
6. Festival/Event Lawn / Great Lawn - infrastructure-at-edge logic and empty-event
   state, avoiding a permanently rendered crowd or stage.
7. Campus Central Quad / Naturalized Meadow - cross-campus desire lines and social
   nodes, with academic buildings compiled separately.
8. Urban Beach / Family Splash - sand/deck/water-play transition and shade kit.
9. Velodrome / Open Air - banked track geometry; needs a dedicated curved-surface
   implementation before promotion.
10. Mountain Bike Park - terrain-aware trail graph and jump modules; defer until
    the terrain-flattening/earthwork interface is validated.

Large venue archetypes such as indoor velodromes, concert pavilions, marinas and
community boathouses were not selected for this batch because their dominant
buildings must remain separate building LEGO families. Seasonal/event families
were also ranked below year-round parks until the viewer has an explicit state
switch instead of permanently baking temporary tents, markets or crowds into
the park model.
