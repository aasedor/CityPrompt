# Suggested public-road connections

Initiative: `codex/public-road-suggestions`, based on `fb413abc3`.
This extends the existing boundary snapping and live 3D street preview. No
catalogue assets, user projects, render providers or paid credits are changed.

## Student behaviour

- While drawing an eligible motor street in the 3D globe, the app loads nearby
  OpenStreetMap roads through an authenticated, read-only endpoint.
- Bring either route endpoint within 10 m of an eligible estimated road edge.
  A cyan marker names the road and the full 3D draft shows the proposed approach.
- Finish drawing to accept. Disable the public-road checkbox to keep ordinary
  site-edge snapping, or hold Alt to bypass all snapping for that finish.
- The accepted route retains its explicit outside-site connection and original
  mapped target label through saving/reopening. Duplicating a street clears that
  association. Existing route editing and server geometry validation still apply.
- Lookup failure leaves manual drawing available with a retry button. Looking up
  roads never changes the site boundary, context revision or scene freshness.

## Bounds and data limitations

This is a conceptual connection aid, not a surveyed junction designer. Road
widths/edges can be estimated and map coverage can be incomplete. Imagery remains
the student's visual alignment reference; the feature does not infer roads from
Google tile pixels or modify those tiles' road geometry.

The first release supports motor streets 3–30 m wide, one external endpoint,
and the existing bounded outside-site connection contract (at most 30 m outside,
less than half the route). It filters mapped roads for complete geometry, eligible
surface-street classes and access restrictions, and avoids segment ends. Transit,
bridges, canals and pedestrian-only routes retain their specialist/manual workflow.
Sites over 1.5 km across, polar locations and truncated map responses do not
receive automatic suggestions. Network queries have bounded timeouts.

The proposal is checked against authored obstacles plus mapped buildings and
closed water polygons. For an explicitly cleared site, only the portion of a
mapped building inside the site is disregarded. Exterior portions remain blockers.
No cadastral parcel dataset is available: neighbouring land ownership, unmapped
obstacles, open waterway lines and surveyed grades are not certified. Complex
junctions, signals, crossings and kerb rebuilding are not generated by this feature.
The legacy 2D editor does not receive this new suggestion UI.
