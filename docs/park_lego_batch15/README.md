# Park LEGO Batch 15

Batch 15 is the first variant-closure pilot. It takes the ten Batch 14 parent families from one executable anchor each to all four catalogue variants: 40 mapped variants total, including 30 new reference-owned PBR skin bundles.

This is intentional shared-family reuse rather than generic styling. Every selection has its own compiler profile, appearance-kit id, planting/program identity, component vocabulary, and exact source image. The live assembly receives the selected variant and changes both material and program logic where the archetype demands it:

- parking distinguishes standard, green-infrastructure, urban/structured and landscape-screened arrangements;
- airfields distinguish a parallel-runway major hub, regional airport, general aviation field and mid-size airport;
- equestrian sites distinguish boarding, working-stable, two-ring competition and modern-academy programs;
- golf distinguishes links, parkland, desert and modern championship landscapes;
- driving ranges distinguish single-tier, entertainment, parkland-pavilion and modern two-tier conditions;
- multisport sites distinguish community fields, stadium/grandstands, dome reservation and track-and-field precincts;
- retail parking distinguishes big-box apron, landscaped lot, gravel overflow and plaza frontage.

Large garages, terminals, hangars, stables, clubhouses, fieldhouses, domes, grandstands and storefronts remain reservation footprints for the separate building pipeline. Park-owned field geometry, runways, ramps, paddocks, parking rows, targets, markings, lighting, planting and furniture are generated in the park layer. No people, AI draping, image API calls or Meshy calls are used.

The coverage checkpoint moves from 380 to 350 unmapped variants. The ten parents in this pilot are now `complete_mapped`, while the remaining 117 parents retain at least one exact executable anchor and await the same variant review.

The live City Prompt pilot used one park at a time beside a separately compiled Parisian building. Three materially distinct selections persisted exact `public_realm_lego` recipes and reported `1 buildings · 1 parks · 0 streets`, `Assembled 1 · No family 0 · Skipped 0`, `compiled`, and the no-drape archetype-owned ground-system statement. The gravel trial also caught and corrected a dark material multiplier that had hidden the reference albedo.
