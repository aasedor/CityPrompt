# City Prompt five-park LEGO trials v2

These five live City Prompt trials were compiled sequentially into the same
single park zone. Only one park was present at a time, so the programs could
not overlap. The separate building LEGO family remained outside the park zone.
No Master Planner, people, or AI ground render was used.

| Trial | LEGO family | Tested footprint | Reference-derived material roles |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Inclusive Accessible Playground | `park_inclusive_playground_v0` | 54 x 44 m | paver, coloured safety surface, lawn, planting, timber |
| Natural Exercise Dog Park | `park_dog_archetype_v0` | 84 x 54 m | paver, lawn pens, planting, timber fencing, stone |
| Timber Water Play Splash Pad | `park_splash_pad_v0` | 34 x 29 m | wet safety surface, concrete, lawn, planting, timber |
| Community Garden Allotments | `park_community_garden_v0` | 54 x 54 m | planting soil, paver, lawn, timber beds, service surface |
| Forest Adventure Nature Play | `park_nature_play_v0` | 44 x 34 m | earth, sand, lawn, planting, stone, timber |

The review images are live Three.js/City Prompt screenshots, not Blender
renders. `cityprompt-five-parks-v2-mobile.png` is the combined mobile review
sheet.

## Review outcome

The inclusive playground, splash pad, community garden, and nature-play
families read clearly as their intended archetypes and retain visibly varied
terrain. The dog-park program is complete, but its five-pen organization and
ground hierarchy still read more diagrammatically than the other four. It
should receive a dedicated composition and material-refinement pass before it
becomes a production-quality archetype benchmark.
