# Park LEGO Batch 17

Batch 17 closes the remaining variants for ten activity-led families:
pickleball, caged soccer, running tracks, outdoor fitness, baseball/softball,
cricket, nature play, inclusive playgrounds, pump tracks, and splash pads.

Each variant keeps the regulation or whole-program modules of its reviewed
anchor family while receiving an explicit material skin, planting structure,
and program identity derived from its own catalogue card. Oversized parcels
repeat complete courts, fields, pens, or activity modules instead of stretching
one element. People in the source photographs remain scale references only;
the LEGO output contains no people or embedded large buildings.

The comparison sheet pairs all forty catalogue cards with their six-role PBR
material palettes, including the ten retained anchor identities.

City Prompt acceptance testing used the urban-contemporary splash pad alone
beside the separately compiled Parisian Boulevard building. It compiled to the
explicit splash-pad family and urban skin on the approximately 40 m by 31 m
parcel. The trial also confirmed two fidelity rules: variant skins use neutral
material tinting so their source albedo remains visible, and authored material
role names inside full-park GLBs are rebound to the selected six-role skin pack.
