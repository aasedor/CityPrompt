# Practical classroom readiness plan — 20 September 2026

The user clarified the goal: students should be able to build their own designs
and create great visuals without glaring errors or inaccuracies. Perfection is
not required. This is the immediate target before expanding the remaining
building, street and park catalogue; it supersedes the previous priority on fine
foundation/rail joins and comprehensive accessibility work.

Construction detailing, tiny seams, comprehensive code/accessibility assessment,
exhaustive rare cases and mobile support can wait unless needed by an advertised
classroom feature. Maintain honest concept limitations. Do not claim accessibility
or asset/publication approval from a working classroom scene.
