# Remaining drawn bridges implementation plan

**Goal:** Convert the nine remaining imported bridge styles into source-derived, two-endpoint local authoring variants with live geometry and compact occupied envelopes.

**Architecture:** Keep original fixed source identities readable. Extract intentional bridge-owned modules with exact source/material provenance, then use a trusted native street programme for rigid ends, repeatable bays, bounded span deformation and complete landscape companions. Preserve the V, S and ring topologies instead of replacing them with straight generic decks.

**Tech stack:** Python/trimesh extraction and source checks; TypeScript/Three.js native street renderer; FastAPI trusted native recipe validation.

**Spec:** User approval to adapt spans to two drawn points, show 3D while drawing and after placement, and remove broad surrounding scenery. The existing timber pilot and `docs/ARCHETYPE_RUNTIME_INTEGRATION.md` define shared preview, save and capture behaviour.

## Constraints

- Immutable original source GLBs and historical saved variants; new exact module hashes and identities.
- Local pilots until each exact variant has runtime evidence. Preserve production publication guards.
- Source materials and distinctive structures; no full-context stretch, sliced plants, floating bearings or implied ground access where endpoints are elevated.
- Two source-derived grade approaches for the ring; explicit source-footprint stair derivative for V bridge access.
- No paid generations, purchases, deployments, broad cleanup or unrelated edits.
