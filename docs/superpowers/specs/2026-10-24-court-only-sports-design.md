# Court-only sports archetypes

Students need individual courts they can combine into their own athletic parks.
Add basketball, tennis, pickleball and beach volleyball as separate local pilot
choices in Parks / Play and sport. Keep the existing complete parks available.

Each assembly includes the native-size playing surface, markings, hoops or net,
and the clear playing reserve. It has no fence, planting, benches, lights,
pergolas or surrounding garden. Move, rotate and repeat are supported; stretching
the court is not. Use the existing native park placement and hash-bound GLB
pipeline. Build procedural geometry, with no paid generation.

Basketball is the first pilot. Inspect its exported GLB and source renders before
building the remaining finite three. Verify the exact exported geometry and the
local student placement/save/reload flow. Keep publication and deployment local.
Deliver clearly labelled source renders and UI evidence to the established Drive
review folder, with a verified delivery manifest.
