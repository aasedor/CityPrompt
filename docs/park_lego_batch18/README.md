# Park LEGO Batch 18

Batch 18 closes all four variants for ten specialty and contemplative park
families: disc golf, bocce/pétanque, outdoor climbing, mini golf, beach
volleyball, pollinator meadows, urban orchards, bioswales, sculpture gardens,
and labyrinth/meditation gardens.

Each variant retains the parcel-adaptive whole-program grammar of its reviewed
anchor while receiving an explicit appearance kit, planting/program identity,
and six-role PBR skin derived from that variant's own catalogue references.
The renderer keeps regulation equipment and distinctive three-dimensional kit
parts intact, neutralizes tint on textured materials so reference albedo is not
lost, and uses people in the references only as scale evidence. LEGO output
contains no people, AI drape, or embedded large buildings.

The comparison sheet pairs all forty catalogue cards with their material-role
palettes, including the ten retained anchor variants. The one-park-at-a-time
City Prompt trial used the Modern Park Court bocce variant on a 39.7 m by
31.2 m polygon beside a separately compiled Parisian Boulevard building. It
remained monochrome until Generate to 3D, compiled the exact family and skin
with zero AI-drape calls, and flattened the Google tiles below the park.
