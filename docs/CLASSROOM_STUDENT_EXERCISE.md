# Your first neighbourhood

**Time:** about 35 minutes. **You need:** the project link and login supplied by
your instructor, a desktop or laptop, and a mouse or trackpad.

Design a small neighbourhood on the vacant Currie parcel. Include **four to six
buildings, one street and one park or teaching garden**. Make it a place you would
enjoy walking through. Choose where buildings belong, how people enter them, and
where neighbours gather. Let the surrounding roads and buildings guide your plan.

You will hand in **one exact 3D image and three sentences explaining your design**.
You can complete the exercise without paid image generation. Open **Guide** in
the design toolbar whenever you need help; close it to try a step.

## 1. Choose your site — 5 minutes

Use your own project. In **Site**, choose **Draw site boundary**. In **Top view**,
click around the empty parcel between the straight eastern road and the curving
western road. Follow its irregular shape, leaving the existing roads and buildings
outside. Press **Enter** to finish. For this exercise, choose **Clear site for
redevelopment** under **Site ground**, then **Save changes**. This replaces the
existing surface inside your boundary with a level site and enables landscaping.
Choose **Confirm site & design**.

If your instructor supplies a blank project with the site already prepared,
review its outline and start below. Tell the instructor you used that starting
point. Do not edit someone else's finished example.

## 2. Build your neighbourhood — 12 minutes

Start with a street so your buildings can face it. Open **Streets**, search for
**Calgary local street**, and choose that card. Click a short route with a bend,
then press **Enter**. Select the street and enable **Connect to a public road**.
Wait for **Drawings saved**, then drag its white end point to the visible edge
of the existing road. Check from
above and from a lower view; stopping somewhere outside your site is not enough.

Open **Buildings**. Search for **Infill homes** and choose the matching **Detailed
3D** card. Place several beside the street. Try **Place another** on the opposite
side. Add a **Beltline brick mixed-use mid-rise** if its size suits your plan.
Keep the starting plot sizes for your first placements. Check the paths between
the doors and sidewalks, and leave useful outdoor space between buildings.

Open **Parks** and search for **Teaching demonstration garden** or **Neighbourhood
park**. Place it close to the sidewalk and check its entrance path. Keep its
starting size so there is room for its features.

If a placement or rotation overlaps another plot, move into clear space and try
again. For a garden, leave room to turn it before bringing its entrance close to
the sidewalk. You can move it with its **Move** handle.

## 3. Explore an alternative — 5 minutes

Select a building and drag its **Move** handle. Try changing its position or
orientation to improve the street or garden. Use white corners to resize, or the
orange handle to rotate where offered. A larger infill plot can add whole houses;
it does not stretch one house.

Use **Undo**, then **Redo**. Check that both return the layout you expect. Decide
which version you prefer. Check entrances, sidewalks and the park again.

## 4. Finish and reopen — 5 minutes

Open **Site → Review site boundary**. On a cleared site, select a landscape
preset, choose **Generate 3D Site Landscape**, then **Apply landscape**. Presets
do not use image-generation tokens. Leave **Custom ground treatment** off for
this exercise. If you edit the layout later and landscaping disappears or needs
updating, generate and apply the preset again.

For an exposed edge on a prepared, level site, open **Review ground**, keep the
saved level unchanged, and enable **Close gaps at site edges** once measurements
are available. Apply and inspect the result. If a control remains unavailable or
the ground looks wrong, record what happened and ask your instructor for help.

Wait for **Drawings saved** and **3D saved**, then reload the project. Check that
your buildings, street, park and landscape return. If saving needs attention,
resolve the message before reloading.

## 5. Present your idea — 8 minutes

Use **Focus plan**, right-drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. Choose an angle showing
the street, buildings and park together, then **Render this view → Image → Export current 3D view · free**.
When the preview opens, choose **Download render**. Open the downloaded PNG and
check that it shows the design you made. Submit that file, not a screenshot of
the app controls.

Write three sentences:

1. How does your neighbourhood fit the surrounding place?
2. How can someone walk from a building to the park?
3. Which change improved your design, and why?

AI image styles are optional after this exercise. Compare an AI image with the
exact view before presenting it; an attractive image can still change a design.

**Before handing in:** the road meets the existing road, entrances and paths are
clear, the scene survived reload, and the downloaded image matches your project.
Tell your instructor about any step that felt confusing, slow or frustrating.
